### AI & I by Every: The AI Sandwich — Where Humans Excel in an AI World

**Kieran Klaassen**, GM of Quora and creator of the Compound Engineering framework, on where humans fit as AI handles more of the execution layer.

**English summary:**

There's a mental model that's been reshaping how the team at Every thinks about working with AI coding agents. It's called the AI sandwich — and the metaphor is surprisingly sticky. The AI is the filling in the middle, responsible for the actual execution: writing code, designing, running tests, iterating. Humans are the bread on top and bottom. We frame the problem at the beginning — set the bounds of what we're trying to solve and why — and then we provide the final polish at the end, the judgment call of whether it feels right, whether it's beautiful enough, whether it's ready to ship. The middle is largely solved. The work phase executes. The review phase catches most errors. What remains is irreducibly human: the frame-setting at the start, and the taste-driven polish at the end. One quote that captures it: *"If you want it to be your own, you need to say yes or no at some point. You cannot fully automate everything. It's maybe a little bit like art."* The deeper argument: language models are generic by nature — they're a super-intelligence kept in a box with no awareness of your specific context. True craftsmanship requires deep tuning to the exact problem and the exact form you're trying to make. That's why humans stay in the loop at both ends. The compound engineering framework (plan → work → review → compound) formalizes this: the "compound" step feeds learnings back into the repo so agents don't repeat mistakes, which is what lets one engineer ship like a team of five.

**中文翻译：**

Every 团队在和 AI 编程 agent 协作过程中，摸索出一个令人印象深刻的mental model——AI 三明治。AI 是中间那层馅，负责执行：写代码、做设计、跑测试、迭代。人类是两片面包，在开始时设定框架——明确要解决什么问题、为什么——在结束时提供最终打磨，判断"感觉对不对"、够不够美、能不能交付。中间层已基本被自动化。工作阶段自动执行，review 阶段捕捉大部分错误。真正不可替代的是人类的部分：开头设定框架，结尾凭借品味做判断。一句原话点睛：*"如果你想让一样东西真正属于你，你必须在某个节点 say yes or no。你无法把一切都自动化。这有点像艺术。"* 更深的论点是：语言模型本质上是通用的——它们是一个被关在盒子里的超级智能，对你的具体上下文一无所知。真正的工艺需要对问题和形式有深刻的 tuning，这只有人类能做到。Compound Engineering 框架（计划→执行→review→复合）将这一点形式化："复合"步骤把学到的经验反馈到代码库，让 agent 不再犯同样的错误——这就是一个工程师能像五人团队一样交付的原因。

### Ryo Lu (@ryolu_), Design Lead at Cursor

**English summary:**

"Overcooking" is the new term for a pattern AI amplifies: when the cost of adding drops to near zero, people add everything that's individually justifiable but collectively incoherent. A dashboard with sparklines on every number, confirmation modals on every action, and animated illustrations on every empty state — each decision made sense to someone, but together it's chaos. The deeper point: most "new ideas" are repackaging of something that already exists at a more fundamental level. AI makes this worse because it can build a whole feature in minutes. The fix requires saying no — which is harder now, not easier, because there's always one more prompt that could add something. The whole has a core. When you overload it, that gravity weakens.

**中文翻译：**

"过度烹饪"（overcooking）是 AI 加剧的一种新现象：当添加功能的成本趋近于零，人们会加入每一个单独看来合理但整体混乱的东西。一个 dashboard 每个数字都有 sparkline，每个操作都有确认弹窗，每个空状态都有动画插图——每个决定对某个人来说都合理，但放在一起就是混乱。更深的论点是：大多数"新想法"不过是以新皮肤重新包装已有概念。AI 让这更糟，因为它可以在几分钟内构建一个功能甚至整个概念。解决方法是说"不"——但这在 AI 时代反而更难，因为总有一个新 prompt 可以再加点什么。整体有一个核心，当你过度填充它，这个重力就减弱了。
