### Marc Andreessen (Co-Founder @ a16z)

**"80-Year Overnight Success": Why This AI Moment Is Different**

In a wide-ranging Latent Space conversation with Swyx and Alessio, Andreessen offered his most complete framework yet on why the current AI moment is unlike previous AI winters. His core metaphor: we're living through an "80-year overnight success." ChatGPT, o1, OpenClaw appear as sudden breakthroughs — but they're drawing on neural network research from 1943, the Dartmouth AGI conference of 1955, the AlexNet breakthrough of 2013, and the Transformer in 2017. Researchers who dedicated their lives to this field often died never seeing it work. The payoff is arriving now.

Andreessen enumerated four fundamental capability breakthroughs that are all working simultaneously: **(1) LLMs** — foundation models that understand and generate; **(2) Reasoning** — o1 and r1 demonstrated that chain-of-thought actually generalizes to real-world tasks; **(3) Agents** — OpenClaw proved that LLM + shell + file system + markdown + cron = a self-extending agent that can modify its own code; **(4) RSI** — recursive self-improvement, where AI improves AI. "If I were 18, this is what I would be spending all my time on."

Yet Andreessen offered a sober counterpoint: the real world is "messy, complicated, and doesn't change overnight." Licensing requirements for hairdressers in California (900 hours), dock workers blocking automation, government agencies operating with minimal oversight — these institutional barriers mean AI adoption will be slower than technologists expect, but more permanent than pessimists fear.

"The dangerous words in investing are: this time is different." — Andreesen argues the actual dangerous phrase is its opposite: "assuming it will fail because it failed before."

马克·安德森（a16z 联合创始人）

**"80 年积累的一夜成名"：为什么这次 AI 浪潮与众不同**

在 Latent Space 与 Swyx、Alessio 的深度对话中，安德森提供了他迄今为止最完整的框架，解释为什么当前的 AI 时刻不同于以往的人工智能寒冬。他的核心比喻：我们正经历一场"80 年积累的一夜成名"。ChatGPT、o1、OpenClaw 看起来像是突然的突破——但它们建立在 1943 年的神经网络研究、1955 年的达特茅斯 AGI 会议、2013 年的 AlexNet 突破以及 2017 年的 Transformer 之上。毕生致力于这一领域的研究者往往在有生之年未能见证它真正发挥作用。现在，回报终于到来了。

安德森列出了同时运行的四项基础能力突破：**(1) LLM**——理解并生成内容的基础模型；**(2) 推理**——o1 和 r1 证明了思维链实际上可以泛化到真实世界任务；(3) **Agent**——OpenClaw 证明了 LLM + shell + 文件系统 + markdown + cron = 一个能自我扩展、可修改自己代码的 Agent；(4) **RSI**——递归自我改进，即 AI 改进 AI。"如果我 18 岁，这就是我愿意倾注全部时间的事情。"

然而安德森也提出了冷静的反面观点：现实世界"混乱、复杂，不会一夜改变"。加州理发师需要 900 小时许可认证、码头工人阻止自动化、政府机构以极低监督运营——这些制度性障碍意味着 AI 普及将比技术专家预期的慢，但也比悲观者恐惧的更持久。

"投资中最危险的话是：这次不同。"——安德森认为真正危险的说法恰恰相反："因为以前失败过就认为这次也会失败。"

### Aaron Levie (CEO @ Box)

**Enterprise AI: From Chatbots to Agents — What IT Leaders Are Actually Doing**

Levie spent a week meeting with IT and AI leaders from banking, media, retail, healthcare, consulting, tech, and sports. His synthesized takeaways:

- **From experimentation to automation**: enterprises are moving away from "let a thousand flowers bloom" AI pilots toward targeted workflow automation with specific ROI
- **Tokenmaxxing**: with OPEX budgets locked for the year, companies are running internal "shark tank" processes to ration compute to highest-value use cases
- **Change management is the #1 challenge**: most enterprise workflows weren't designed for agents — integration work is far larger than the AI itself
- **Headless software wins**: enterprises will kick out vendors that don't make their software callable by any agent through skills/MCP/CLIs
- **Multi-agent world = interoperability crisis**: as companies run multiple agents, cross-system communication becomes a paramount engineering problem
- **Engineers won't be "writing" software — they'll be orchestrating the systems that automate work**: Levie's sharpest observation: "The most powerful ways to use agents is more 'technical' than prior eras of software. Skills, MCP, CLIs... may be simple concepts for tech, but in the real world these are esoteric concepts that will require technical people to help bring to life in the enterprise."

### Garry Tan (President & CEO @ Y Combinator)

**The Golden Rule of Agentic Engineering: Fat Skills, Fat Code, Thin Harness**

Tan distilled his most important lesson from a year of agentic engineering into a single architectural principle:

> "Push smart fuzzy operations that humans do into **Markdown Skills** (Fat Skills). Push must-be-perfect deterministic operations into **code** (Fat Code). Keep the **harness** thin."

This is separation of concerns for AI systems: let the LLM handle ambiguity and judgment calls, let hardcoded logic handle anything where failure is costly. The harness — the orchestration layer — stays minimal.

**Agent 工程的黄金法则：肥技能、肥代码、薄 Harness**

谭从一年的 Agent 工程经验中提炼出最核心的架构原则：

> "把人类做的智能模糊操作放进 **Markdown Skills**（肥技能）——把必须精准零失误的确定性操作写进**代码**（肥代码）——让 **harness** 框架保持精简。"

### Claude Blog (Anthropic)

**Project Glasswing: Preparing Your Security Program for AI-Accelerated Offense**

Anthropic published a comprehensive security guide tied to their Project Glasswing announcement. The core thesis: AI models are rapidly reducing the cost, time, and skill required to find and exploit vulnerabilities. Within 24 months, vast numbers of bugs that have sat unnoticed in code for years will be found by AI models and chained into working exploits.

Key recommendations:

- **Patch gap collapsing to 24 hours**: the window between patch publication and exploit availability is shrinking dramatically. Automate patch deployment and reboots for internet-facing systems
- **Order-of-magnitude increase in vulnerability volume**: plan your intake, triage, and remediation tracking for 10x more findings
- **The counterintuitive Jevons paradox for security talent**: better AI security tooling increases demand for security professionals — AI automates the finding, humans still needed for containment, remediation, and architectural judgment
- **"AI vulnerability scanning" is the single highest-impact action**: deploy an isolated agent to scan your own code with the same capability an attacker would use
- **Patch generation is now viable**: frontier models can propose patches for SAST findings; reviewer's job shifts from "write a fix" to "verify the proposed fix"
- **For individuals without a security team**: enable auto-updates, prefer managed services, use passkeys, enable free security tooling (GitHub Dependabot, secret scanning, CodeQL)

### Claude Blog（Anthropic）

**Project Glasswing：为 AI 加速攻击时代准备你的安全体系**

Anthropic 发布了与 Project Glasswing 公告配套的全面安全指南。核心论点：AI 模型正在迅速降低发现和利用漏洞所需的成本、时间和技能。在 24 个月内，大量在代码中沉睡多年未被注意的漏洞，将被 AI 模型发现并串联成可用的漏洞利用程序。

关键建议：

- **补丁窗口压缩至 24 小时**：补丁发布与漏洞利用出现之间的时间窗口正在急剧缩小。对面向互联网的系统自动化补丁部署和重启
- **漏洞数量级增长**：为你的接收、分类和修复跟踪流程规划 10 倍以上的漏洞发现量
- **安全人才的 Jevons 悖论（反直觉）**：更好的 AI 安全工具实际上会增加安全专业人员的需求——AI 自动化了发现过程，但人类仍需要负责遏制、修复和架构决策
- **"AI 漏洞扫描"是单次行动中影响最大的措施**：部署一个隔离的 Agent，用与攻击者相同的能力扫描你自己的代码
- **补丁生成现在已经可行**：前沿模型可以为 SAST 发现提出补丁；审查者的工作从"写一个修复"变为"验证提出的修复是否正确"
- **对没有安全团队的个人**：启用自动更新、优先使用托管服务、使用 passkey、启用免费安全工具（GitHub Dependabot、secret scanning、CodeQL）
