### Mike Krieger on Agent Native Products

**EN**

Krieger's sharpest product thesis: the difference between a good AI product and a great one is whether every *primitive* of the product can be exercised by the AI—not just the surface features. Claude Code is the canonical example. He argues this principle needs to be baked in from day one, not retrofitted. Products built in 2024 still show the seams; Claude Code (2025 vintage) gets it right because it was designed with this mindset from the start. The next frontier: having AI that can modify its own harness, extending the paradigm further.
