### Unsupervised Learning — Episode 84

**OpenAI Chief Scientist: Continual Learning is the Core, RL Beyond Code, and Future Alignment Directions**
*(OpenAI 首席科学家：持续学习是核心，RL 超越代码的路径，以及未来对齐方向)*

**The Takeaway**: OpenAI's chief scientist sees the biggest unlocked opportunity in long-horizon AI research agents — models that can work autonomously for days, discover novel math ideas, and collaborate with scientists in the loop. The key insight: don't supervise the chain of thought; the magic is in what the model reveals when it's thinking privately.

**核心结论**：OpenAI 首席科学家认为最大的未释放机会在于长时跨度的 AI 研究 Agent——能够自主运行数天、发现新的数学思路、与科学家协作的模型。核心洞察：不要监督思维链；真正的魔法在于模型在私下思考时透露出的东西。

**Key Insights / 关键要点**:

1. **Coding agents already transforming work**: OpenAI internally uses Codex for the majority of coding work. In a few months, expect models that work autonomously for a couple of days with much higher quality output.

2. **RL beyond coding**: The next frontier is applying RL to domains (medicine, law, finance) where "success" is harder to define. The solution: teach models to evaluate partial progress and work for longer time horizons.

3. **Math as North Star**: Math benchmarks serve as a general benchmark for reasoning because success is easily verifiable yet arbitrarily hard. IMO-level problems are now being solved; the next milestone is research-level mathematics.

4. **AI for Science is real**: GPT-5.2 Pro has already produced genuinely novel math ideas in collaboration with research mathematicians. The model is not just pattern matching — it's discovering new approaches.

5. **Chain-of-thought monitoring is the key alignment tool**: Don't supervise the chain of thought during training. The reasoning traces reveal the model's true motivations, which is critical for understanding future super-intelligent models.

6. **On AGI timelines**: "We're not that far." The models aren't smarter than humans in every way, but they are transformative. Society needs to be prepared to make tradeoffs and potentially slow down depending on what emerges.

> "The biggest role that focusing on math benchmarks has played for us is as a general benchmark and North Star for how to improve the model."

> "Allowing models some private space for their chain of thought is actually a very powerful paradigm for being able to interpret what the model is doing."
