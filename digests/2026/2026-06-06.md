### OpenAI's Dan Roberts: Why AI Can Now Make Discoveries

**The Takeaway:** OpenAI's RL Foundations lead Dan Roberts explains why AI just proved it can do original science — not by pattern-matching, but by exploring contrarian paths for days. RL is now "the cake," language is the essential grounding layer, and AI's scientific breakthroughs are only the beginning.

Dan Roberts leads the Foundations of Reinforcement Learning team at OpenAI, coming from a PhD in theoretical physics (MIT, researching quantum gravity and black holes). His journey from studying how information behaves in black holes to building the world's most powerful AI systems isn't random — it's the same first-principles thinking applied to intelligence itself.

**On the Erdos math breakthrough:** What made the OpenAI result remarkable wasn't just the answer — it was the method. The model assumed the conjecture was *false* when everyone believed it was true. To do that, you need extraordinary conviction to persist down a long, uncertain calculation path, combined with expertise in algebraic number theory. That's an extremely rare human skill combination. The model brought it together.

**On why RL works now:** "One of the key moves in physics is to study if the spherical cow is sufficient to describe the thing you care about — if yes, you did a good job." RL works now because language gives us that spherical cow: all of human scientific knowledge, mathematical proof, and behavioral reasoning is encoded in language. Models think in language, and RL teaches them to think *better* at test time. RLHF was the first step; test-time compute with RL is the second.

**On "sucking supervision through a straw":** The 1-bit-per-10000-tokens critique has merit in terms of sample efficiency. But the question isn't whether you could supervise better — it's whether you can supervise *at all*. The breakthroughs in coding and science show that RL on language priors accomplishes things that weren't possible any other way.

**On the future:** Dan jokes he predicted 9 years to Einstein-level AI (doubling time of autonomous work vs. the 8 years Einstein needed for general relativity). He's skeptical he'll be right — "something will break before that" — but expects continuous math and science breakthroughs, and AI increasingly automating AI research itself.

**Key quote:** "I feel really excited that we will get to really answer a lot of fundamental questions in the field of science that we care about with the aid or the models being the driving force. And so that's just really thrilling."

**OpenAI 强化学习基础团队负责人 Dan Roberts：AI 刚刚证明了它能做原创科学——不是靠模式匹配，而是靠连续数天的反向探索。** RL 是"蛋糕本身"而非装饰，语言是智能的 grounding layer，AI 的科学突破只是开始。Dan 从研究黑洞中的量子信息到构建前沿 AI 系统，用的是同一套第一性原理思维。他指出：OpenAI 解决 Erdos 问题的关键不是答案本身，而是方法——模型敢于假设"这个被广泛相信的猜想是错的"，并有韧性沿着漫长、不确定的路径走到底。RL 之所以现在有效，是因为语言提供了"球形牛"——所有人类科学知识、数学证明和行为推理都编码在语言中，模型以语言为思维媒介，RL 教会它们在推理时思考得更深。

### Anthropic Engineering: Claude Code Quality Report (Postmortem)

Following user reports of degraded Claude responses over the past month, Anthropic identified and fixed three separate bugs in Claude Code (not the API):

1. **Default reasoning effort change (March 4):** Changed default from high to medium to reduce latency — but users preferred intelligence over speed. Reverted April 7.
2. **Cache optimization bug (March 26):** A supposed efficiency improvement to clear stale thinking after 1+ hour idle accidentally cleared thinking on *every subsequent turn*, making Claude progressively forgetful and repetitive. Fixed April 10.
3. **System prompt verbosity limit (April 16):** "Keep text between tool calls to ≤25 words, final responses ≤100 words" instruction degraded coding quality. Reverted April 20.

All three issues affected different slices of traffic on different schedules, creating the appearance of broad inconsistent degradation. Usage limits have been reset for all subscribers. As of the investigation, when provided complete repository context, Opus 4.7 found the cache bug — Opus 4.6 did not.

**Anthropic Engineering 博客：详细披露 Claude Code 三个 bug 的根因和修复：** ①默认 reasoning effort 从 high 调至 medium（已回滚）；②缓存优化 bug 导致 thinking history 每个 turn 持续丢失（已修复）；③系统 prompt 字数限制影响代码质量（已回滚）。所有受影响的订阅用户使用限制已重置。调查期间，Opus 4.7 在获得完整代码库上下文后发现了该 bug，Opus 4.6 则没有。

### Anthropic Engineering: Scaling Managed Agents — Decoupling the Brain from the Hands

Anthropic published a deep dive on the architecture of Managed Agents. The core insight: treating the agent loop (brain), execution sandbox (hands), and session log (memory) as separate interfaces that can each fail or be replaced independently — just like operating systems virtualized hardware. This decoupling dropped p50 time-to-first-token by ~60% and p95 by >90%. The security model ensures credentials are never reachable from the sandbox where Claude's generated code runs (bundled with resources or held in external vaults). MCP tunnels now allow agents to reach internal databases, private APIs, and ticketing systems via a single outbound connection with no public endpoints.

**Anthropic Engineering 博客：Managed Agents 架构详解——将 agent loop（大脑）、执行沙箱（手）和 session log（记忆）解耦为独立接口。** 核心思路：OS 通过虚拟化硬件创建了能跨越数十年的抽象，Managed Agents 同样将各组件虚拟化，使每个组件可独立失败或替换。TTFT p50 下降约 60%，p95 下降超过 90%。安全模型保证凭证从不暴露在代码执行沙箱中。MCP tunnels 支持通过单一出站连接访问内部数据库、私有 API 和工单系统。
