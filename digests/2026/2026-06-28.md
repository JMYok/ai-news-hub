### Memory and Continual Learning: Engram's Dan Biderman and Jessy Lin

**The Takeaway:** Engram is building AI that learns company-specific knowledge deeply into model weights, potentially cutting inference costs by 100x compared to brute-force context stuffing.

Dan Biderman and Jessy Lin are co-founders of Engram, a research lab tackling what they see as the next frontier bottleneck in AI—making models remember and learn new information the same way humans do, rather than constantly re-reading everything. If you're deploying AI in any business context, this is the talk that explains why your models will soon need their own training programs.

The core problem: current AI is incredibly smart but deeply forgetful. It knows general facts but struggles with your specific workflows, priorities, and institutional knowledge. Today's fix is "context engineering"—stuffing more tokens into prompts. But as Jessy points out, "The amount of tokens we will all collectively generate is going to be in the tens of millions of tokens per day soon. Just keeping it and searching through it is going to be expensive and confusing for the models."

Engram's solution is to train models directly on your workspace data—using adapter fine-tuning techniques like LoRAs and prefixes—to bake company knowledge into the weights. Their partners (Notion, Microsoft, Harvey) get models that understand context the way a five-year employee would, rather than just reading files at test time.

The payoff is dramatic: Dan describes cases where a trained model answers in 100 tokens what a general model would need 100,000 tokens to handle. That's not incremental improvement—it's an order-of-magnitude shift in efficiency and capability.

They'll need white-box model access (works easiest with open-source), but the vision is clear: every team's AI will eventually learn continuously, internalizing what matters and externalizing what doesn't.

核心问题：当前的AI非常聪明，但却极易遗忘。它知道通用事实，但在处理你特定的业务流程、优先级和机构知识时却力不从心。当前的解决方案是“上下文工程”——在提示中塞入更多token。但正如杰西所指出的：“我们所有人集体生成的token数量很快将达到每天数千万个。仅仅保存和搜索它们，对模型来说将是昂贵的且令人困惑的。”

Engram的解决方案是在你的工作空间数据上直接训练模型——使用LoRA和前缀等适配器微调技术——将公司知识嵌入权重中。他们的合作伙伴（Notion、微软、Harvey）获得的模型能够像在公司工作五年的员工那样理解上下文，而不是在测试时仅仅读取文件。

收益是显著的：丹描述了一些案例，经过训练的模型用100个token回答的问题，通用模型需要100,000个token才能处理。这不是渐进式的改进——而是效率和能力的数量级跃升。

他们需要白盒模型访问权限（与开源模型配合使用最为容易），但愿景很清晰：每个团队的AI最终都将持续学习，将重要的东西内化，将不重要的东西外化。
