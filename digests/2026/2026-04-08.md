### Mistral Team — Voxtral TTS (Audio Research + Chief Scientist)

**English Summary:**

Mistral's audio research lead Pavan Kumar Reddy and Chief Scientist Guillaume Lample appeared on Latent Space to unveil Voxtral TTS — their first speech generation model built entirely in-house. The model uses a novel autoregressive flow matching architecture with a custom neural audio codec that converts audio into 12.5 Hz latent tokens (semantic + acoustic). The key insight: speech generation's core challenge isn't *what* to say but *how* to say it — the same words can be inflected in infinite ways, and Flow Matching models this distribution far better than Depth Transformers. Lample made a bold case for Mistral's modular strategy: instead of one expensive general model, deploy small specialized models (3B for TTS, OCR, etc.) fine-tuned on private enterprise data. This approach is 10x cheaper, runs on-premise, and outperforms closed-source generalists on specific tasks. He also hinted at AI for science partnerships (e.g., with ISM Elementary) as the next frontier.

**中文概要:**

Mistral 音频研究负责人 Pavan Kumar Reddy 和首席科学家 Guillaume Lample 亮相 Latent Space，发布 Voxtral TTS——首个完全自研的语音生成模型。该模型采用全新的自回归 Flow Matching 架构，配合自研神经音频编码器，将音频转换为 12.5 Hz 隐变量 token（语义 + 声学）。核心洞察：语音生成的本质挑战不是"说什么"而是"怎么说"——同一句话有无限种表达方式，Flow Matching 在建模这种分布上远优于 Depth Transformer。Lample 还强调了 Mistral 的模块化策略：用小而专的模型（3B TTS、OCR 等）替代昂贵的大模型，配合企业私有数据微调，成本可降低 10 倍，支持本地部署，在特定任务上甚至超越闭源通用模型。他同时透露，Mistral 正与 ISM Elementary 等机构合作探索 AI for Science 作为下一个前沿方向。

### Aaron Levie (Box CEO)

**English Summary:**

Levie pushed back on the narrative that AI agents will make human work obsolete. When agents complete tasks for you, work simply "moved up a layer of abstraction" — the new work is figuring out what to tell the agent, providing proper context, intervening when it goes off-task, reviewing output, and incorporating it into something else. Any one of these steps being off produces useless results. The longer the task horizon, the more planning and review dominate. "You are now the editor. A manager. A producer. The taste matters, understanding the craft matters, and the skills ultimately matter." AI hasn't changed the nature of work — it's just changed which parts humans do.

**中文概要:**

Levie 反驳了"AI Agent 将让人类工作变得多余"的说法。当 Agent 替你完成任务时，工作只是"上移了一层抽象"——你需要做的是：确定给 Agent 什么指令、提供恰当的上下文、在它偏离时介入纠正、审核最终产出、并将其整合到更大的流程中。任何一个环节出问题，最终结果就是废品。任务周期越长，规划和审核阶段占比越高。"你现在就是编辑、是管理者、是制片人。品味很重要，对专业的理解很重要，最终技能也依然重要。"AI 没有改变工作的本质，只是改变了人类负责的部分。
