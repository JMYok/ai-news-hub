### Guillermo Rauch (Vercel CEO)

**英文：**
Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch announced over 20 product improvements across Dashboard and CLI to help customers strengthen their security posture, including easier MFA setup, environment variable audits, activity logs, and more. He clarified a critical misconception: deletion (e.g., of an env var, project, or account) does not imply key rotation. Rotating keys means *invalidating* the previous value with the vendor/service you're using and getting a new one—if you only delete the resource on the Vercel side, the associated key can "live on" with the other provider and be misused.

**中文：**
Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch 宣布在 Dashboard 和 CLI 上推出了超过 20 项产品改进，以帮助用户加强安全态势，包括更简便的 MFA 设置、环境变量审计、活动日志等。他澄清了一个关键误解：删除（如删除环境变量、项目或账户）并不等同于密钥轮换。轮换密钥意味着 *使*之前的值在供应商/服务端失效，然后获取新密钥——如果你只在 Vercel 端删除资源，关联的密钥可能仍然在对方提供商那里"存活"并被滥用。
