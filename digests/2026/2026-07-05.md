## 🎙️ PODCAST

**MAD Podcast with Matt Turck** — *"Cloudflare CEO: The Internet's Business Model Is Dead"*
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UN47z_opfmo

Cloudflare CEO **Matthew Prince** dropped one of the most consequential data points of the year: in the first half of 2026, bot traffic officially surpassed human traffic on the internet. Cloudflare's radar data shows bots now exceed humans — and Prince's projection is stark: **1,000x more bot traffic than human traffic within five years**, driven almost entirely by AI agents shopping, researching, and browsing on behalf of users.

The core crisis he articulates: **the internet's business model for 28 years — advertising — is broken in an agentic world**. Bots don't click ads. Something has to replace it, and nobody knows what yet. Prince's bet: microtransactions (402 payment required), processing hundreds of millions per second — two orders of magnitude beyond Visa's capacity.

On security: "For the next two years, we're going to see a Log4j-like vulnerability every single week." AI finds vulnerabilities faster than humans can fix them — but two years from now, software will be "massively better" as a result. Internally, Cloudflare's code-review agent caught an issue that caused a visible cliff in their incident rate.

On the Cloudflare AI stack: Workers (isolate-based serverless for AI), AI Gateway (audit, guardrails, cost routing), and the broader network effect — now processing half a billion requests per second. 93% of their R&D team now uses AI coding tools. One skeptical senior engineer tried to debunk AI coding, spent a month with it, and reported: "I am now 100x more productive." That engineer was a 10x engineer to begin with.

On the layoffs: they cut 20% of staff not because the business was failing, but because the roles (middle managers, measuring functions) aren't needed in an AI-first org. Span of control expanding from 6:1 to 12:1. "The cruelest thing you can do is wait" — better to let people go now while the job market is still relatively open, than flood it in 6-12 months when every company does the same.

On the early Cloudflare customer story: the first week they launched, over a thousand Turkish prostitution websites signed up for the free tier, followed by every humanitarian organization in the world. The Turkish escort DDoS pattern later traced back to an Iranian student who eventually got recruited into the Iranian military cyber unit — and attacked US banks including JPMorgan. "We protect everyone, and that helps us learn from even the weird corners of the internet."

关于裁员：裁减 20% 员工，不是因为业务下滑，而是 AI-First 组织不再需要那些岗位（中间管理层、测量型职能）。管理幅度从 6:1 扩展到 12:1。"最残忍的事是等待"——现在放手比 6-12 个月后所有人一起裁员要好得多。

Cloudflare 最早的客户故事：上线第一周，一千多个土耳其风月网站注册了免费版，随后全球所有人道主义组织也涌进来。追踪发现，后来攻击摩根大通的伊朗黑客，最初只是攻击那些土耳其网站的学生。"我们保护所有人，这也让我们能从互联网的每个奇怪角落学习。"
