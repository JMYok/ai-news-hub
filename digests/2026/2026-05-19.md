**"The ability to READ is incredible — arguably much more useful on a day-to-day basis."**

He sees AI agents as "Thomas's English muffins" that get into every nook and cranny of organizations, because they don't care about the specific form of inputs and outputs, and can adapt to any workflow.

**"AI coding agents will fundamentally expand — not replace — the demand for technical talent."**
Levie shared a contrarian take: while people fear AI will eliminate coding jobs, the reality is demand for technical talent is expanding into every industry. Speaking with a Fortune 500 pharma CEO, he learned they need MORE technical talent now than five years ago — not less. His key point: those who can steer AI agents, evaluate their work, fix mistakes, and integrate results into workflows will always be the most potent users. The experienced developer with AI beats the novice with AI every time.

### AI & I by Every — "Claude Code Can Be Your Second Brain"

Noah Breyer 展示了用 Claude Code 作为真正"第二大脑"的完整系统。他的设置：地下室服务器 + Obsidian（本地笔记，Markdown 文件）+ Tailscale VPN，手机上运行 Claude Code 做深度研究和代码推送。他的核心工作流：开启 Claude Code 在整个 vault 上工作 → 打开一个"思考伙伴"子 agent，只负责提问和记录，不写任何内容 → 每天让 AI 总结当天的研究进展。

几个反直觉的洞察：1) AI 的阅读能力比写作能力更有用，我们产出内容远不如消费/处理信息频繁；2) 所有模型都会默认跳到"帮你写"的模式，必须明确说"我在思考模式，不是写作模式"；3) 用 Probabilistic 计算机（会对你同一问题给出不同答案）非常奇怪，但我们每天都在和人类这样互动——只是还没习惯用这种新词汇。

**Noah Breyer showed off a complete "second brain" system with Claude Code + Obsidian running on a home server accessible from his phone.**
His core workflow: start Claude Code on the entire vault, spawn a "thinking partner" sub-agent that only asks questions and takes notes without writing, and have the AI summarize daily research progress. Key contrarian insights: (1) AI's reading ability is underutilized compared to its writing; (2) All models default to "help me write" mode — you must explicitly say you're in thinking mode; (3) Using probabilistic computers that give different answers to the same question is strange, but humans deal with that every day — we just haven't built the vocabulary yet.
