### The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — "State of Enterprise AI 2026: Aaron Levie on Tokenmaxxing, Rise of Headless, and AI-Proofing Your Job"

**The Takeaway:** AI's pace of progress is so fast that it makes the prior breakthrough obsolete — the real enterprise challenge isn't AI capability, it's the organizational capacity to absorb and implement it.

**核心结论：** AI 进步的速度已经到了上一次突破会使上上次突破过时的程度——企业落地的真正挑战不是 AI 能力本身，而是组织消化和实施它的能力。

**Summary:**

Box CEO Aaron Levie has a front-row seat to how AI is deployed inside the world's largest enterprises. His TLDR: we are incredibly early. Most companies are still rolling out chat systems, while the next wave — agents — is already at the door.

Box CEO Aaron Levie 坐在企业 AI 落地的最前排。他的判断：我们仍然处于非常早期的阶段。大多数公司刚完成聊天系统的部署，而下一波——Agent——已经敲门。

On token costs: the jump from chatbot pricing ($20/user/month) to agentic compute is stark. A single coding task can consume $1,000 of compute. This isn't sustainable under old models, and companies are scrambling to build FinOps for the marketing team and the sales team — something the cloud era never had to solve.

关于 Token 成本：从聊天机器人定价（$20/人/月）到 Agent 计算的跳跃是惊人的。一个简单的编码任务可能消耗 $1,000 的计算资源。这在旧模式下行不通，企业正在为营销团队和销售团队建立 FinOps——这是云时代从未解决过的问题。

The biggest bottleneck is data, not models. Agents need secure, reliable access to enterprise content with the right entitlements. That sounds simple, but most enterprises have contracts in five different places, data spread across 30+ systems, and access controls that weren't designed for machine-to-machine access. This is a 5-10 year transformation problem.

最大的瓶颈是数据，不是模型。Agent 需要安全、可靠地以正确权限访问企业内容。这听起来简单，但大多数企业的合同散落在 5 个不同地方，数据分布在 30+ 个系统里，访问控制并非为机器间访问而设计。这是一个 5-10 年的转型问题。

The internal FDE (Field Deployment Engineer) role is emerging as the critical function — technical people embedded in business teams whose job is to wire up agentic workflows. This won't be a one-time implementation; it'll be ongoing work as models change and capabilities improve.

内部 FDE（Field Deployment Engineer）角色正在成为关键职能——技术人才嵌入业务团队，负责将 Agent 工作流连接起来。这不是一次性的实现；随着模型变化和能力提升，这将是持续性的工作。

Levie's contrarian view: the "AI psychosis" phase (where you believe one employee + agents = entire company) gives way to reality — maintaining agents takes real work, and the last mile of high-stakes knowledge work always needs a human in the loop.

Levie 的反直觉观点："AI psychosis"阶段（相信一个员工 + Agent = 整家公司）会被现实取代——维护 Agent 需要真实的工作，高风险知识工作的最后一英里始终需要人类在场。
