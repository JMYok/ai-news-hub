### Aaron Levie (Box CEO)

不要把"任务"和"岗位"混淆。AI 可以自动化岗位中的某些任务，但岗位的定义会随之扩展——人们会做更多任务、更高质量的工作、或者转向尚未自动化的任务类型。编程、法律、销售、市场营销都是如此。中小型公司以前负担不起完整开发团队，现在可以借助 AI agents 承接更大的软件项目；以前负担不起营销agency的小公司，现在可以雇佣一个人完成 agency 的工作量。

Aaron Levie, CEO of Box, warns against confusing task completion with job elimination. Even as AI automates individual tasks within a role, the definition of the job expands to do vastly more of those tasks, at higher quality, or pivot to tasks not yet automated. This applies to coding, legal, sales, and marketing. Small businesses that couldn't afford a full dev team can now take on larger software projects with agents; those that couldn't afford a marketing agency can hire one person to do what an agency did before.
