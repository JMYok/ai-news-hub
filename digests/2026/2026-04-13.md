**The Agentic Economy: Programmable Money as the Native Infrastructure for AI Agents**

In a landmark No Priors conversation, Allaire laid out why the AI agent revolution needs a fundamentally different financial layer. All with cryptographic provability. Allaire's thesis: as AI agents proliferate, they'll need machine-native financial infrastructure that's globally interoperable, instant, and programmable — and USDC + ARC is the candidatest implementation.

"We have the opportunity to create essentially new social, political, and economic organizational structures. And in many ways, we have to." — Jeremy Allaire

**AI Won't Reduce the Legal Profession — It Will Expand It**

In a contrarian post, Levie argued that AI automation in law will likely *increase* demand for lawyers. Three mechanisms: (1) more people will ask legal questions, many leading to actual lawyer engagement; (2) AI will generate an explosion of new and exotic legal terms requiring human review; (3) every industry adopting AI creates entirely new IP, privacy, and regulatory compliance challenges — new law. Historical precedent: between the PC and the internet, active ABA attorneys grew from ~400,000 (1975) to ~1,375,000 (2025).

"When we make professions more efficient and automated, often demand for them goes up not down." — Aaron Levie

### Peter Steinberger (Polyamorouse ClawFather, Core Contributor @ OpenClaw)

**OpenClaw's Next Frontier: Strict Mode + Native Codex as Pluggable Harness**

Steinberger unveiled two significant architectural experiments in OpenClaw targeting the "GPT is lazy" problem:

1. **Strict Mode** (`agents.defaults.embeddedPi.executionContract = "strict-agentic"`): Forces GPT-5.x to keep working — read more code, call tools, make changes — or explicitly return a real blocker. No stopping at "here's the plan."

2. **Native Codex as Harness**: OpenClaw harnesses are now plugins, making it trivial to swap the default Pi harness for Anthropic's SDK or other custom implementations. Using Codex as the harness (`agents.defaults.embeddedHarness = { runtime: "codex", fallback: "none" }`) means Codex owns threads, resume, compaction, and app-server execution. This could increase agentic mode reliability for longer-horizon tasks, at the cost of potentially weakening personality.

### Garry Tan (President & CEO @ Y Combinator)

After 3 months of releasing open source used by tens of thousands of agentic engineers daily, Tan distilled the core architectural lesson: **Memory is Markdown. Skills are Markdown.
