### AI & I by Every: The AI Model Built for What LLMs Can't Do

Logical Intelligence CEO Eve 认为，当前的 AI 投资泡沫正在自我强化——数百亿美元已经涌入 LLM，数据中心、芯片厂商、Big Tech 形成了一个无法打破的利益共同体。但在她看来，LLM 架构存在根本性缺陷：它在"猜词"，而非"理解"。

Eve 打了个比方：你让一个 LLM 在旧金山导航，它只能一次走一个方向、不能回头，像一个只能往前看的迷路的行人。而 EBM（能量基础模型）能"俯瞰全局"——同时评估所有可能的路径，选择最优路线，而非猜错了再重试。

EBM 为什么更重要？因为它解决的是 LLM 无法解决的关键任务：代码形式化验证、芯片设计、医疗诊断、金融交易——这些领域不允许幻觉，输出必须可证明正确。Eve 透露，一些大厂已经在内部研发 EBM，但整个行业还需要时间消化这个范式转移。

她认为未来不是"用 EBM 替代 LLM"，而是让 EBM 成为 LLM 的"验证层"：语言任务交给 LLM，空间推理和关键决策交给 EBM，两者协同。

> "We're trying to use literature department everywhere. And I'm like, hey, we don't have to do everything through language. It's a matter of resources — if you have infinite money and you don't care about timescale, sure, but if your AI controls circuits and can't wait even a second, this form of AI is not suitable."

Logical Intelligence CEO Eve argues that the current AI investment bubble is self-reinforcing — billions have poured into LLMs, and Big Tech, data centers, and chip vendors have formed an unbreakable circle of mutual dependency. But in her view, the LLM architecture has a fundamental flaw: it guesses tokens rather than understands.

Eve uses an analogy: asking an LLM to navigate San Francisco is like a person who can only walk one direction at a time with no ability to turn back. An EBM (Energy Based Model) can "see the whole map" — evaluating all possible paths simultaneously and picking the optimal route, rather than guessing wrong and retrying.

Why does this matter? Because EBM solves what LLMs fundamentally cannot: code formal verification, chip design, medical diagnostics, financial transactions — domains where hallucination is unacceptable and outputs must be provably correct. Eve reveals some major tech companies are already building EBMs internally, but the industry needs time to消化 this paradigm shift.

Her vision: not "replace LLMs with EBMs" but make EBM the "verification layer" on top of LLMs — language tasks to LLM, spatial reasoning and critical decisions to EBM, working together.
