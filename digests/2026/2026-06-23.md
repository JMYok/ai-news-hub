### How Anthropic Uses Claude Fable 5 With Mike Krieger

## English Summary

**The Takeaway:** Claude Fable 5 isn't just a more powerful model—it requires a completely new mental model where you start treating AI as a teammate you can delegate meaningful, multi-step work to overnight.

Mike Krieger, co-founder of Instagram and current head of Anthropic Labs, offers a rare ground-level view of what it's actually like building with Fable 5 day after day. For him, the shift wasn't gradual—it was immediate and disorienting. "I feel like a total newbie again because I feel like the way that I am prompting or even thinking about decomposing a task is really out of date now with this model."

The biggest change? Krieger now treats Claude less like a sophisticated autocomplete and more like a teammate he can hand complex tasks to before a flight, then trust it'll handle failures autonomously overnight. "I'm gonna write a scaffolded back end for it for now. I'll document that. I'll go all the way through. And then when it comes back online, I'll fix it." That's not hyperbole—it's his actual workflow now.

He admits that using Fable 5 for quick questions feels wasteful. "It's like using a rocket launcher to kill a mosquito," he says with a laugh. So he's started using effort levels, dialing Fable down to medium for minor tweaks and reserving full power for architectural planning and complex delegation.

His practical advice for knowledge workers: embrace the long-horizon modality. Set up context, walk away, and let the model execute chunks of work while you focus on human alignment. The productivity unlock isn't asking better questions—it's rethinking what you delegate entirely.
