### Training Data: From SEO to Agent-Led Growth with Profound CEO James Cadwallader

**The Takeaway:** AI isn't just changing the front door of the Internet — it's replacing the person walking through it. The real paradigm shift is that AI agents, not humans, are now consuming and synthesizing web content on behalf of users. Marketers who understand this are redesigning content specifically for agents to discover and consume.

James Cadwallader, co-founder and CEO of Profound, joined Training Data to explain why AI search is the biggest platform shift in marketing history — bigger than the transition from print to digital. His core thesis: **the Internet has stayed the same; it's who is using the Internet that changed.**

In the old world, a human consumer used blue links to discover your website and click through. In the new world, an AI agent uses a similar index, discovers your brand and products, and then comes back through the door maintaining that relationship with the consumer. "When you ask ChatGPT a question or Gemini a question, what you're essentially doing is asking an agent to go and crawl the Internet on your behalf."

**Key Insight 1: Content designed for agents will never be consumed by a human.**

The biggest misconception in this new paradigm is that it's "just SEO." But in SEO, you were building content designed to be picked up by an algorithm but fundamentally consumed by a human. In this new world, you're building content that is designed entirely to be discovered and consumed by an agent.

**Key Insight 2: Each platform eats different content.**

Gemini will lean heavily on YouTube content because Google owns YouTube. ChatGPT pools from Reddit for consumer and LinkedIn for B2B. Claude has been updating its classifiers to be more sensitive to real-time information — using the web more to answer questions.

**Key Insight 3: Agents consume 100x more of the Internet.**

A single showerhead query triggered ChatGPT to use **65 different web pages** to answer. Humans are constrained by cognitive energy and patience. Agents have infinite bandwidth and will use Moore's Law to consume ever more of the Internet to build rich answers.

**On "slop" — AI-written content:** Cadwallader thinks this is a red herring that will be quickly disproven. A New York Times experiment found 53% of readers in a blind test preferred AI-written content. "AI is more than capable of writing high quality content." The real question for brands is: how do you tell a superintelligent being something it doesn't already know, when it's been trained on the entire Internet? The answer: **original insight from humans — the fleshy API between reality and the Internet.**

**On Dead Internet Theory:** Cadwallader thinks we could see a "dead Internet" outcome in the next 3 years. If humans stop clicking into websites, the advertising business model breaks, the economics of the Internet break, and incentives to create editorial content disappear. The second-order effect: every AI lab will eventually vertically integrate with a social media network. Social media will become more human over time — the precious place where AI can understand what's actually going on in the world.

**On AI advertising:** OpenAI rolling out ads will be the most effective form of advertising ever. "AI is so good at synthesizing and personalizing language to the needs of Sonya in that exact moment." The ad unit of the future? You'll just prompt it — "build a system prompt as an ad campaign."

**Profound CEO James Cadwallader 在 Training Data 播客中提出了一个令人深思的命题：AI 改变的不是互联网的门，而是穿过那扇门的人——从人类变成了 AI Agent。**

他的核心观点：

- **内容是为 Agent 设计的**：以前 SEO 的内容最终是被人类消费的，现在的 content designed for agents will never be consumed by a human
- **每个平台吃的内容不同**：Gemini 偏好 YouTube（Google 拥有它），ChatGPT 偏好 Reddit/LinkedIn，Claude 正在更新分类器变得更敏感
- **Agent 消费互联网的广度是人类的 100 倍**：一个淋浴头查询触发了 65 个不同网页的调用。随着推理成本下降，这个数字只会增加
- **关于"slop"（AI 垃圾内容）**：这是会被快速证伪的红鲱鱼。53% 的读者在盲测中更喜欢 AI 写的内容。真正的品牌问题是：当你的内容被训练在 entire Internet 上时，如何告诉一个超级智能体它不知道的东西？答案是：**来自人类的原始洞察——现实与互联网之间的 fleshy API**
- **关于 Dead Internet Theory**：三年内可能看到 dead Internet 的出现——如果人类不再点击网站，广告商业模式崩溃，内容创作激励消失。每个 AI 实验室最终会垂直整合一个社交网络，社交媒体会变得更像人类
- **关于 AI 广告**：AI 广告将是有史以来最有效的广告形式。未来广告就是一个 system prompt
