### Yann Dubois (OpenAI Post-training Frontiers 负责人，斯坦福 Alpaca 联创)

Yann Dubois 在 MAD Podcast 上分享了 GPT-5.5 背后的核心逻辑：

AI 进步看起来像跳跃式突破，实际上是持续积累——直到去年12月我们终于跨过了"可靠性门槛"，模型再也不会每隔两分钟就出错一次。以前模型跑 10 分钟必然出错，现在可以连续跑数小时。这是"感觉像跳跃"的本质。

强化学习从数学/编程竞赛（可验证答案）迁移到真实工作（代码审查、复杂任务），是这波加速的真正原因。工具没变，变得是我们去掉了那些"简化假设"。

更大的模型虽然生成更多 token，但并行度更高，推理反而更高效。GPT-5.5 大多数任务比 4o 快两倍，同时质量更高。

Harness 不会消失——模型再强，last mile 问题永远存在：权限、上下文、领域知识，这些需要 harness 来解决。短期内 AI 公司和专业 harness 公司是共生关系。

Continual learning 还没解决——三年前以为 AI 会自动学会个性化，三年后还没做到。模型从 Day 0 开始就比大多数新员工有用，但人类会持续学习而模型不会。

> "Pre-training is like going to a library with all the books. Post-training is talking to an expert who read them all."
> "The last mile in different verticals will always exist — I would highly encourage people to continue working on that."

Yann Dubois, who leads the Post-training Frontiers team at OpenAI and co-authored Stanford Alpaca, joined the MAD Podcast to explain what's really behind GPT-5.5:

AI progress feels like a step function jump, but it's actually continuous — we just crossed the "reliability threshold" last December. Before, models would fail roughly every 10 minutes; now they can run for hours without errors. That's the real reason it feels like a breakthrough.

Reinforcement learning migrating from math/coding competitions (verifiable answers) to real work (code review, complex tasks) is the true driver of this acceleration. The tools didn't change — we just removed the "simplifying assumptions."

Larger models generate more tokens but parallelize better, making inference more efficient overall. GPT-5.5 is ~2x faster than 4o on most tasks while being higher quality.

Harnesses won't disappear — no matter how powerful the model, the last mile problem always exists: permissions, context, domain knowledge. AI companies and specialized harness companies are in a symbiotic relationship short-term.

Continual learning remains unsolved — three years ago we thought AI would automatically learn personalization; three years later, it hasn't. Models start more useful than most new employees on Day 0, but humans keep learning while models don't.

> "Pre-training is like going to a library with all the books. Post-training is talking to an expert who read them all."
> "The last mile in different verticals will always exist — I would highly encourage people to continue working on that."

### Aaron Levie (Box CEO)

Levie 指出：从 coding agent 到知识工作 agent，最大的瓶颈不是模型智能，而是能否拿到正确的数据和权限。当 context 来自代码库且用户是技术人员时，agent 很好用；但到了知识工作场景，context 分散在遗留系统、访问权限映射错误、甚至存在于人脑中的 tribal knowledge 里。这就是为什么 FDE（Foreign Data Environment）和新系统集成商现在这么火。

Aaron Levie, CEO of Box, pointed out that the biggest bottleneck moving from coding agents to knowledge work agents isn't model intelligence — it's whether you can get the right data and permissions. When context comes from a code base and users are technical, agents work great. But in knowledge work scenarios, context is scattered across legacy systems, mismapped access controls, and tribal knowledge in people's heads. This is why Foreign Data Environments and new system integrators are booming right now.
