### The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — OpenAI Board Member Zico Kolter on the Real Risks of Frontier AI

**The Takeaway:** AI safety does NOT improve automatically with scale — you must explicitly train for robustness. Bigger models get more capable, not more secure. True safety requires dedicated safety training, layered defenses (the "Swiss cheese model"), and a whole-chain security view from the model layer to the ecosystem layer. Zico also dropped a mind-blowing fact: GPT-5.5-level code is just 200-300 lines of Python — all complexity emerges from data, not architecture.

**Key Quote:** "AI systems are incredibly simple, incredibly simple. That entire set of code, probably two to 300 lines of Python code. That blows my mind. The entire complexity of an AI system evolves from the data they're trained on."

**核心洞察：** AI 安全不会通过 scaling 自动改善——必须显式训练鲁棒性。更大的模型能力更强，但不一定更安全。真正的安全需要专门的安全训练、多层防御（"瑞士奶酪模型"），以及从模型层到生态层的全链路安全视角。Zico 还有一个令人震惊的发现：GPT-5.5 级别的代码只有 200-300 行 Python——所有复杂性都从数据中涌现，而非来自架构。
