### Training Data: Knowing What Your Customers Want, All the Time: Listen Labs' Alfred Wahlforss

**The Takeaway:** In the age of AGI, building is getting easier — the real constraint is knowing what to build. Listen Labs has done 1M AI interviews and is now training audience simulations that can predict how specific personas will answer questions with 95% accuracy.

Alfred Wahlforss, CEO of Listen Labs, founded the company after building a viral consumer app (AI avatar creator) that hit 20k users overnight. They discovered that running AI interviews internally to understand churn was more valuable than the app itself — and Listen Labs was born.

Listen Labs is an AI-first customer research platform that runs thousands of voice interviews simultaneously across a panel of 30M participants (from oncologists to software engineers). The AI agent creates interview guides, conducts conversations, and synthesizes recommendations. They now serve 20% of Fortune 500 including Microsoft, Anthropic, Sweetgreen, and NBC.

**On simulation:** After conducting hundreds of thousands of interviews, they can now predict how customers will answer future questions. Alfred tested it personally: he created 100 talk titles for a conference, ran them through simulation, and the top pick was 2x better than the next. When he tested the same against ChatGPT, ChatGPT picked the wrong one. The key differentiator: interview data captures behavioral reasoning, not just preferences — it's structured to understand how people think, not just what they prefer.

**On pricing:** Enterprise work (e.g., speaking to 20 doctors across 8 countries) commands hundreds of thousands. Individual interviews become more affordable, but volume of research grows by 2 orders of magnitude.

**On vertical AI moats:** The eval is everything. When they started, GPT-4 would ask the same question 100 times — 20% eval score. They climbed to 85%. Then they created a harder eval (can the AI skip irrelevant questions, understand screen context) and dropped back to 20%. That's the advantage: proprietary evals that you climb continuously.

**Key quote:** "As we get closer to AGI, it will be easier to build things, but the hard part will know what to build. And that's what we're building at Listen."

**Listen Labs CEO Alfred Wahlforss：AGI 时代，build 会越来越容易，真正的瓶颈是 know what to build。** 他们已完成 100 万次 AI 访谈，正在训练 audience simulation —— 输入一个人的信息，可预测其回答，准确率达 95%。Alfred 用自己的演讲标题实验验证：100 个选项中 simulation 选出的最优比 ChatGPT 选的更有效。核心护城河：proprietary eval —— 他们从 20% 爬到 85%，又创建更难的新 eval 回到 20%，这个爬坡过程就是垂直 AI 的壁垒。经典语录："AGI 让 build 更容易，但 know what to build 才是hard part。"
