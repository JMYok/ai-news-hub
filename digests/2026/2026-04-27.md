### Bill McDermott (CEO, ServiceNow) — Podcast: No Priors

**The Takeaway:** Language models give advice; workflow platforms close the loop. Trying to replicate an enterprise platform with LLMs costs 10x more — and when the model fails, there's no one to call.

Bill McDermott, CEO of ServiceNow and one of the most recognizable figures in enterprise tech, sat down with No Priors to discuss leadership in the age of AI and what enterprise customers actually want. His central argument cuts through the SaaSpocalypse noise: LLMs are excellent at advising, but enterprise work requires deterministic, multi-system workflows that actually close cases. "AI thinks, but workflow acts."

He did the math: for a single application on ServiceNow, replicating it with language models would cost **10x more** — factoring in human capital, GPU factories, and token costs that materially affect the LLM company's business model. And when the model makes a mistake? "People never forgive software for making a mistake." Meanwhile, ServiceNow handles 85 billion+ workflows in flight and 7 trillion transactions on the platform, integrating with 800+ systems of record. The switching cost and data context are a genuine moat.

On AI agents entering the workforce, McDermott predicted 2.2 billion agents in the next couple years — outnumbering people in many companies. His advice to leaders: "Don't look for the escape hatch. This is the moment when leaders really matter."
