### Training Data: From SEO to Agent-Led Growth (James Cadwallader, ProFound CEO)

**James Cadwallader 是 ProFound 联合创始人兼 CEO，ProFound 是一个 AI 时代的营销平台，帮助企业在 ChatGPT、Claude 等 Agent 的搜索结果中优化品牌曝光。** 他在节目中提出了一个令人深思的观点：互联网本身没变，但"走进门的人"变了——不再是消费者，而是替他上网抓取信息的 AI Agent。这意味着营销的本质从"让人类找到你"变成了"让 AI Agent 推荐你"。

他指出了当前最大的误区：把 Agent 搜索当成 SEO 的延续。事实上，SEO 的逻辑是抢前 5 个链接，而 AI Agent 会深入互联网的长尾，使用远超人类的页面数量来回答一个问题（他本人用 ChatGPT 买花洒时，AI 参考了 65 个网页）。这意味着营销内容需要面向"超级智能、带宽无限"的 Agent 设计，而不仅仅是人类读者。不同平台的 AI（Gemini 偏向 YouTube、ChatGPT 偏向 Reddit、B2B 偏向 LinkedIn）信息来源差异巨大，理解这个生态是新的必备能力。

他提出了一个激进预测：**未来 3 年内可能出现"死互联网"结局**——当人类不再点击网站，广告收入归零，优质内容生产激励消失，AI 转而依赖品牌方自己发布的 README。但社交媒体（Reddit、X）将成为人类保持原创性的"生物区"，与 AI 形成新的共生。

> "It's gone from a nice to have to a must have... It would be like not using the Internet. Yeah. I think it would be like, hey, we're gonna stick to print and TV. Yeah. Thanks. I don't believe in this Internet thing."

James Cadwallader is Co-Founder and CEO of ProFound, a marketing platform for the AI era that helps companies understand how they appear in AI search results from ChatGPT, Claude, and other agents. He made a thought-provoking observation on the show: the Internet itself hasn't changed—what has changed is who walks through the door. It's no longer consumers but AI agents crawling the web on their behalf. This means marketing has fundamentally shifted from "getting found by humans" to "getting recommended by agents."

He identified the biggest misconception: treating agent search as just another SEO channel. In reality, SEO is about ranking in the top 5 links, while AI agents dive deep into the long tail of the internet, referencing far more pages than any human would. When he bought a showerhead using ChatGPT, it referenced 65 web pages. This means marketing content needs to be designed for "superintelligent agents with infinite bandwidth," not just human readers. Different AI platforms also draw from wildly different sources—Gemini leans on YouTube, ChatGPT on Reddit, B2B on LinkedIn—making platform-specific strategy essential.

He offered a provocative prediction: a "dead Internet" scenario could unfold within 3 years, where humans stop clicking websites, ad revenue collapses, incentive for quality content disappears, and AI relies increasingly on brand-published READMEs. Social media (Reddit, X) would become "bio zones" where humans maintain originality, forming a new symbiosis with AI.

> "We've now reached a point in marketing where if your marketing team is not using agents, and in particular, ProFound agents, to do marketing... then you are failing. I think it would be like not using the Internet."

### Aaron Levie (Box CEO)

Aaron Levie 是 Box CEO，他在推特上深入分析了 **Forward Deployed Engineer（FDE）角色在 AI Agent 规模化落地中的不可替代性**。他指出，当企业购买 Agent 产品时，本质上购买的不仅是软件工具，而是"技术实际完成的工作流程本身"——这需要深入理解客户业务领域、打通系统连接、提供变更管理支持，远超传统软件实施范畴。他将这种模式类比为专业服务公司而非传统软件厂商，认为这为系统集成商和咨询公司创造了巨大的新业务机会，同时也解释了为什么"AI 会抢走所有工作"的叙事过于简单化——**人类的专业知识和对特定领域的深度理解，在 Agent 落地的最后一公里中仍然不可或缺**。

Aaron Levie, CEO of Box, offered an in-depth analysis of why the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) role remains irreplaceable as AI agents scale in enterprises. He argued that when a company buys an AI agent product, they're not just buying a software tool—they're buying the actual workflow being done by the technology itself. This requires deep domain expertise, wiring up customer systems, and change management support, far beyond traditional software implementation. He likened this model more to a professional services firm than a traditional software vendor, creating massive new practice areas for system integrators and consulting firms. This also explains why the narrative of "AI taking all jobs" is too simplistic—**human expertise and deep understanding of specific domains remains indispensable in the final mile of agent deployment**.

Zara Zhang is a former Harvard engineer and highly prolific open source contributor with 70,000+ GitHub Stars on GStack. She broke down how she used Claude to "cut features" when building the Tab Out Chrome extension: realizing Chrome history is locally parseable by LLMs, wanting a New Tab page as the entry point, successfully having Claude customize it, then cutting the LLM-driven tab grouping because it was slow and unreliable.
