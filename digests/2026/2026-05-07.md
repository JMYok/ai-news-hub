### Anthropic Engineering: Claude Code auto mode

**EN:**
Anthropic published a deep dive on Claude Code's new auto mode — a safer way to skip permissions for AI coding agents. The system uses two layers: (1) a server-side prompt-injection probe at the input layer, screening tool outputs before they reach the agent; (2) a two-stage transcript classifier at the output layer (fast single-token filter + chain-of-thought reasoning). On real internal traffic (n=10,000), the full pipeline achieves 0.4% FPR. On real overeager actions (n=52), FNR is 17% — honest number, but still a massive improvement over --dangerously-skip-permissions. The design is reasoning-blind to prevent the agent from talking the classifier into bad calls. Multi-agent handoffs are protected at both delegation and return.

**CN:**
Anthropic 发布了 Claude Code 新增 auto mode 的深度技术解析——一种让 AI coding agents 更安全地跳过权限的方式。系统采用双层防御：(1) 输入层的服务器端 prompt 注入探测，在工具输出进入 agent 上下文前进行筛查；(2) 输出层的两阶段 transcript classifier（快速单 token 过滤 + 链式推理）。在真实流量测试（n=10,000）中，完整管道 FPR 仅 0.4%。在真实越界行为测试（n=52）中，FNR 为 17%——这是诚实的数据，但相比 --dangerously-skip-permissions 仍是巨大改进。设计上对 reasoning 保持盲区，防止 agent 说服 classifier 做出错误判断。多 Agent 交接在委托和返回两端都受保护。

### Training Data: Waymo's Dmitri Dolgov — 20 Million Rides and the Road to Full Autonomy

**EN:**
Dmitri Dolgov has been working on autonomous vehicles since the 2005 DARPA Challenge — over 20 years. Waymo started in 2009 as Google's self-driving project, and it took 18 months for a small team of ~12 to complete their initial goals: 100,000 miles in full autonomy and 10 separate 100-mile routes without intervention. The early days were intense — sleeping at the office, 24/7 execution. The autonomous vehicle hype cycle of 2016-2017 saw many fall by the wayside; Waymo persisted through believing in the mission and understanding the long tail of difficulty. Today they operate in 11 cities, have given 20M+ rides, with 10M of those in just the last 7 months. Their 6th generation hardware is dramatically cheaper, simpler, and designed around rider experience — the Ioniq 6 feels like a living room inside. Safety data: 13x safer than human drivers in their operating cities, preventing a serious injury every 8 days at current scale. Key technical insight: Waymo's foundation model is a multimodal world-action-language model — it understands physics, dynamics, behaviors of other agents, and is controllable as an active participant. They augment end-to-end learning with structured materialized intermediates for validation, closed-loop evaluation, and RL reward functions — going far beyond vanilla end-to-end. A memorable moment: the lidar detected a pedestrian hidden behind a bus by picking up foot movement signals under the bus — "it blew my mind."

**CN:**
Dmitri Dolgov 自 2005 年 DARPA 挑战赛就从事自动驾驶研究——已超过 20 年。Waymo 2009 年作为 Google 自动驾驶项目启动，最初一个约 12 人的小团队花了 18 个月完成最初目标：10 万英里完全自动驾驶里程 + 10 条各 100 英里且无人工干预的路线。早期的日子非常艰苦——睡在办公室，全天候运转。2016-2017 年的自动驾驶泡沫周期让许多参与者倒下；Waymo 靠对使命的信念和对困难长尾问题的清醒认识挺了过来。如今在 11 个城市运营，完成超 2000 万次行程，其中最后 1000 万次仅在过去 7 个月内完成。第六代硬件成本大幅降低、设计简洁、以乘客体验为核心——Ioniq 6 内部空间像客厅。安全数据：在运营城市中比人类驾驶安全 13 倍，按现有规模每 8 天阻止一次严重伤害。核心技术洞察：Waymo 的 foundation model 是多模态 world-action-language model——理解物理、动力学、其他 agent 的行为，并作为主动参与者可控。他们用结构化的 materialized intermediate 增强端到端学习，用于验证、闭环评估和强化学习奖励函数——远超普通的端到端架构。一个令人印象深刻的时刻：激光雷达通过捕捉行人脚步信号检测到了藏在公交车后的行人——"这让我震惊不已。"
