### Ivan Burazin (CEO of Daytona)

Daytona CEO Ivan Burazin 是当前 AI Agent 基础设施领域最被关注的声音之一。他在 MAD Podcast 中分享了一个核心观点：每个 AI Agent 都需要至少一台专属的沙盒（电脑）才能发挥作用。Agent 本质上是数字知识工作者——要完成复杂任务，它们需要自己的机器、自己的账号（甚至自己的电话号码用于 2FA），而不是共享人类的电脑。

Burazin 的关键洞察：当你把 Agent 放在你自己的电脑上并给它银行权限时，它知道所有密码——这是安全噩梦。解决方案是给 Agent 自己的机器，它只能查看数据，无法转账。他将这种"专用计算机"称为 composable computers for AI agents，并认为这将彻底改变我们对云基础设施的认知。

Daytona CEO Ivan Burazin shared his core thesis on the MAD Podcast: every AI agent needs at least one sandbox (its own computer) to operate effectively. Agents are digital knowledge workers who need their own machine, own account, even own phone number for 2FA—rather than sharing a human's computer. The key insight: when you give an agent access to your personal computer with bank credentials, it knows all your passwords—a security nightmare. The solution: give it its own machine where it can only view data, not transfer money. He calls this "composable computers for AI agents" and believes it will fundamentally reshape how we think about cloud infrastructure.

### Aaron Levie (CEO of Box)

Levie 认为 AI 实际上会增强专业化而非替代它。在当下这个"一切都被打乱"的时代，虽然 AI 让人们更容易探索相邻角色（比如 PM 也能生成设计稿），但这种混合是暂时的。随着功能开发速度提升 10 倍，PM 需要花更多时间在客户反馈和产品设计上，工程师需要更深入理解业务目标并构建可扩展系统——两者的工作量都变得更大，专业化的必要性会重新凸显。

Aaron Levie, CEO of Box, argues that AI will ultimately enhance specialization rather than replace it. While AI makes it easier to explore adjacent roles (a PM can now generate a working design), this blending is temporary. As feature velocity increases 10x, PMs need to spend even more time on customer feedback and product design, and engineers need to deeply understand business objectives and build scalable systems—the workload for each actually increases, making specialization necessary again.

### The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — "Why Every AI Agent Needs Its Own Computer" with Ivan Burazin (Daytona)

Daytona CEO Ivan Burazin 在 MAD Podcast 上系统性地阐述了他对 AI Agent 基础设施的思考。他认为过去二十年所有人都在谈"本地开发环境末日"，但开发者说"除非从我冷冰冰的尸体上拿走我的 localhost"。直到 Agent 出现，情况才真正改变——因为 Agent 不需要在意在哪台机器上运行，它们只需要一台属于自己的电脑。

Burazin 将当前的 Agent 技术栈分为几层：模型（大脑）、工具（沙盒、电脑、MCP）、记忆（memory）和编排（orchestration）。其中记忆是当前最未被解决的难题——目前最佳方案是用 Markdown 文件，但模型本身并不会在学习中真正变聪明，每次都是"新模型"而不是"更好的模型"。

他还提到了一个重要预测：继 GPU 短缺之后，全球可能即将面临 CPU 短缺。因为 RL 是当前模型进步的核心路径，而 RL 需要大量 CPU 机器配合 GPU 并行运行。

Ivan Burazin, CEO of Daytona, joined Matt Turck on the MAD Podcast to systematically explain his thinking on AI agent infrastructure. He believes that for the past twenty years everyone has been saying "the end of local hosts" but developers insisted "over my cold dead body." Only with the arrival of agents did this finally change—because agents don't care which machine they run on, they just need a computer of their own.

Burazin breaks down the current agent tech stack into layers: models (the brain), tools (sandboxes, computers, MCP), memory, and orchestration. Memory is the most unsolved problem currently—the best solution uses Markdown files, but models themselves never actually learn smarter during a session; every time is a "new model" rather than a "better model."

He also shared an important prediction: after the GPU shortage, the world may be facing a CPU shortage. Because RL is the core path for current model improvement, and RL requires massive CPU machines running in parallel with GPUs.
