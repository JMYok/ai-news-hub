### Swyx (Latent Space Podcast, DX Tips)

Swyx reflected on his first failed devtools startup and identified false equivalence as the root cause. In strategy and content, it's tempting to go wide rather than deep — "oh we do a, b, c, and d" with equal weight on all of them. But the world is not fair and power laws compound. Most organizations — school systems, bureaucracies, managers, content curators — are not set up for one thing to matter 50x more than the next. His rules: carefully bet on a very small set of things; don't hedge, but keep reversibility; set triggers to monitor if you are wrong; set tests to double down early if you are more right than you thought.

https://x.com/swyx/status/2037969691910848941

---

Swyx 复盘了自己第一家失败的 DevTools 初创公司，发现 false equivalence 是根本原因。在战略和内容上，人们很容易"广撒网"而非深耕——"我们做 a、b、c、d"，所有方向等权重。但世界不公平，幂律在放大差异。大多数组织——学校系统、官僚机构、管理者、内容策展人——都没有为一个方向的价值是另一个方向50倍这种情况做好准备。他的原则：精心押注极小范围的事情；不 hedge，但保持可逆性；设置触发器监测判断是否错误；如果判断正确且超出预期，要设置测试以便尽早加注。

### Zara Zhang (Builder, Follow Builders Creator)

Zhang articulated a fundamental distinction between internet-era products and agent products: a good internet product should work exactly as its creator intended. A good agent product, by contrast, should be able to do things its creator did not think it could do. The test for a great agent is not whether it follows instructions faithfully, but whether it surprises and delights you with capabilities you didn't anticipate. If your agent only does what you expected, it's not yet truly valuable.

https://x.com/zarazhangrui/status/2038144755478237654

---

Zhang 指出了互联网时代产品与 Agent 产品的根本区别：好的互联网产品应该完全按照创造者的预期工作。而好的 Agent 产品，应该能够完成其创造者未曾想到它能做到的事情。衡量优秀 Agent 的标准不是它是否忠实地遵循指令，而是它是否以出乎意料的能力给你带来惊喜。如果你的 Agent 只能做你预期之内的事，它还没有真正发挥价值。
