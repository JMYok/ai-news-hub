### Aaron Levie (Box CEO)

**English:**
Levie breaks down exactly why enterprise agent adoption is 100x harder than personal productivity AI — and why this is the biggest opportunity in AI right now. Enterprise agent rollouts require: secure data access across decades of legacy infrastructure, fine-grained access controls and entitlements, comprehensive monitoring/logging, process documentation that agents can actually use, new human-agent workflow design, and fresh evaluation frameworks. "While it's fun for people to change their personal productivity tools on a dime, it's 100X harder to do this in a business process. The speed of change is a blessing and a curse right now." His conclusion: vertical AI agents that go deep in a specific business domain are uniquely positioned to crack this.

**中文:**

### Training Data — Andrej Karpathy: From Vibe Coding to Agentic Engineering

**English:**
Karpathy's debut on "Training Data" is the most important AI podcast episode this week. He walks through his journey from skeptic to believer after December 2025 — when models became reliable enough that he stopped correcting them and started trusting the system. He introduces "Software 3.0" as a new computing paradigm where prompting replaces code and context is the control lever. The MenuGen example illustrates this viscerally: what took dozens of libraries and pipelines, Gemini does in one image-to-image pass. He discusses "verifiability" as the key property that determines which domains AI automates fastest — and notes the current jaggedness is partly a function of what labs chose to put in the RL training. His sharpest observation: "You can outsource your thinking, but you can't outsource your understanding" — the real bottleneck as AI gets cheap. On agentic engineering specifically: top practitioners are far beyond 10x. And on the animal vs. ghost framing: these are "summoned" intelligences shaped by data and reward functions, not intrinsic motivation or curiosity.

**中文:**
Karpathy 首次亮相"Training Data"是本周最重要的 AI 播客节目。他回顾了自己从怀疑者到信徒的转变——2025 年 12 月之后，模型变得足够可靠，他不再需要修正输出，开始真正信任这个系统。他提出"Software 3.0"作为一种新的计算范式：prompting 取代代码，上下文成为控制杠杆。MenuGen 案例让人直观感受到这一点：原本需要几十个库和流水线的功能，Gemini 一次图生图就完成了。他探讨了"可验证性"这一关键属性——决定 AI 在哪些领域自动化最快——并指出当前的参差不齐一定程度上是实验室选择将什么放入 RL 训练的结果。他最犀利的观点："你可以外包你的思考，但无法外包你的理解"——在 AI 日益廉价的时代，真正的瓶颈在于理解力。关于 agentic engineering：顶尖从业者的产出远超 10 倍。关于"动物 vs. 鬼魂"的框架：这些是被数据和奖励函数"召唤"出的智能，而非由内在动机或好奇心驱动的。
