**The Takeaway:** Satya Nadella's most important thesis: every company's future IP isn't its data or its people — it's their private evals and the harness they've built to climb them. Nadella describes Microsoft's Build conference as fundamentally an ecosystem play: "Can every company, whether AI-native or traditional enterprise, participate as a first-class participant where they can point to AI they create?" Not just consuming AI — creating it. The MAI models they announced signal a shift from "build the best model" to "build the best platform for you to build your own specialist."

The flexibility to offer all three models is the competitive advantage.

### Anthropic Engineering — "How we contain Claude across products"

**核心结论：** Agent 安全有三层：Model（模型层）、Environment（环境层）、External Content（外部内容层）。Anthropic 的经验是：环境层 containment 是根本，模型层 defense 再强也只是 probabilistic，总有漏网之鱼。两次最大的教训——员工钓鱼事件和第三方 allowlist 数据泄露——都是数据通过合法途径 egress，模型层完全无能为力，能堵住它们的只有确定性边界。

Anthropic 把 agent 部署分成三类架构：① ephemeral container（claude.ai 代码执行，gVisor 隔离，blast radius 最小）② HITL sandbox（Claude Code，OS 级沙箱 + auto mode 分类器）③ sealed VM（Claude Cowork，完整虚拟机，完全隔离）。经验：沙箱越严密，对用户的监督要求越低；对技术背景强的开发者可以用 human-in-the-loop，对普通知识工作者必须用绝对边界。

一个关键洞察：egress allowlist 不能只做 destination filter，要做 capability grant。允许访问 api.anthropic.com 意味着允许上传文件到任意 Anthropic 账户——他们的修复方案是在 VM 内部放置 MITM proxy，只放行带有 VM 自有 session token 的请求。定制组件是薄弱环节：hypervisor、seccomp、gVisor 都经受住了考验，出问题的都是自己写的 proxy。

**原文引言：** "The deterministic boundary is what gets hit when everything probabilistic misses."
