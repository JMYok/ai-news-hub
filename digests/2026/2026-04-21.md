### The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — OpenAI Chief Scientist Noam Brown on Continual Learning, RL Beyond Code, and Future Alignment

Noam Brown 是 OpenAI 首席科学家，也是 AI 推理和策略研究领域最具影响力的人物之一。他在播客中分享了大量一手洞察，涵盖 RL 在通用领域的局限性、context learning 的数据效率优势，以及 AI for Science 的最新进展。他透露，模型已能自主解决部分数学研究问题——包括自己博士研究方向的问题，模型在一小时内给出了他本人都很自豪的证明思路。更重要的是，他提出了一个反直觉的结论：很多公司正在构建的 RL pipeline，本质上可能是错误的方向——context learning 才是更数据高效的学习方式，与其花时间训练定制 RL，不如把好例子喂进 context。

**The Takeaway:** 当执行成本趋近于零，产品竞争的本质已从"模型能力"转向"问题定义能力"——理解"该解决什么问题"比"怎么解决"更值钱。

Noam Brown is OpenAI's Chief Scientist and one of the most influential figures in AI reasoning and strategy research. In this wide-ranging podcast, he shares candid insights on RL's limitations in general domains, the data efficiency advantage of in-context learning, and the latest progress in AI for Science. He revealed that models can now autonomously solve math research problems—including those from his own PhD background—generating proof ideas in an hour that he would have been proud to produce over a week. More importantly, he offered a counter-intuitive conclusion: many companies building custom RL pipelines may be heading in the wrong direction—in-context learning is fundamentally more data-efficient, meaning the better move is feeding good examples into context rather than building RL pipelines from scratch.

**The Takeaway:** When execution becomes essentially free, the real competition shifts from "model capability" to "problem definition capability"—knowing what problem to solve is worth more than knowing how to solve it.

### Aaron Levie (Box CEO)

Aaron Levie 认为 AI 不会让大多数岗位消失，反而会让岗位复杂度自然提升。你能做的越多，你就越有能力去解决更大更难的问题；而要真正完成这些更大的任务，专业知识仍然是必需的。一个会用 AI 的工程师远比一个不会用 AI 的非工程师更高效。轻量 App 不再是软件工程的入门门槛，审查合同不再是 paralegal 的定义，剪切视频不再是视频剪辑师的职责——但与此同时，这些岗位需要处理的问题层级已经完全不同了。AI 会让大多数角色随着工具能力一同"升级"，而不是简化。

Aaron Levie argues that AI won't make most jobs disappear—in fact, it will naturally increase role complexity. The more you can do, the more capable you become of tackling bigger, harder problems—and expertise is still required to fully execute those larger tasks. An engineer who uses AI will always be far more effective than a non-engineer trying to build the same software. Building a lightweight app is no longer the definition of getting by in software development. Reviewing a contract is no longer what defines a paralegal. Splicing a video is no longer the definition of a video editor—and yet the complexity of problems these roles handle has entirely shifted upward. AI will naturally cause most roles to grow in sophistication alongside the tools, not simplify them.

### Guillermo Rauch (Vercel CEO)

Guillermo Rauch 披露了 Vercel 近期遭遇的复杂安全事件：一名 Vercel 员工在第三方 AI 平台被攻破后，其 Vercel Google Workspace 账户随后沦陷，攻击者通过一系列层层渗透获取了更多 Vercel 环境访问权限。Rauch 坦言"强烈怀疑攻击已被 AI 大幅加速"——攻击者展现出的速度和深度超出了常规水平。值得注意：Vercel 对所有客户环境变量都做了静态加密，但"非敏感"环境变量的存在为攻击者留下了缺口。建议所有客户轮换密钥、监控 Vercel 环境访问、并正确使用敏感环境变量功能。

Guillermo Rauch disclosed a sophisticated security incident at Vercel: a Vercel employee's account was compromised through a third-party AI platform, leading to their Vercel Google Workspace account being breached, followed by a series of escalations that gave the attacker further access to Vercel environments. Rauch stated he "strongly suspects the attack was significantly accelerated by AI"—the velocity and depth of the attacker's movements exceeded what is typical. Key detail: while Vercel encrypts all customer environment variables at rest, the existence of "non-sensitive" environment variables created a gap the attacker exploited. Recommendation: rotate secrets, monitor Vercel environment access, and properly use the sensitive env var feature.

### Zara Zhang (Builder)

Zara Zhang 认为，随着 AI 能力提升，产品团队应该花更多时间在外部沟通（与用户、客户交流）而非内部沟通。原因有三：1）确定"做什么"将比"怎么做"更值钱，这需要与外部人碰撞灵感；2）团队会越来越小，内部协调成本将大幅下降；3）独立创业者已经在展示一种更极致的模式——整天与客户交流，然后把录音丢给 AI agent，让 AI 决定做什么并执行。她本人观察到，自己身边最优秀的独立创业者朋友，已经在用这种方式运转团队。

Zara Zhang argues that as AI capabilities improve, product teams should spend more time on external communication—talking to users and customers—than on internal communication. Her reasoning: 1) Figuring out "what" to build will be worth far more than building it, which requires sparks of inspiration from external people; 2) Teams will get much smaller, so internal coordination overhead will drop dramatically; 3) Independent entrepreneurs are already showing a more extreme version of this—they spend all day talking to customers, then dump recordings to their AI agents, which figure out what to do and handle execution. She's observed that her most successful indie entrepreneur friends are already running their teams this way.
