### AI & I by Every — "We Automated Everything With AI and Tripled Our Headcount"

**Every grew from 4 to 30 people since GPT-3, and they're hiring more now — not despite AI, but because of it.**

Every's COO Brandon Smith interviews CEO Dan Shipper about his viral piece "After Automation." Shipper's central thesis: AI makes yesterday's expert competence cheap, everyone starts doing expert-level work, that floods the zone with tons of stuff that's close-but-not-quite-right, and then you actually get more demand for experts who can make it actually good. The piece came from Shipper watching Every grow from 4 to 30 people while being "as AI native as it gets" — everyone uses Claude Code and Codex daily. He argues that when you project out to AGI, the fundamental separation between humans and agents remains: agents are built to do things *we* want them to do, they wait for us to decide what matters. Even after AGI, theoretically, it will still be looking back at you asking what to do next.
