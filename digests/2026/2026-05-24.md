**English:**

### Anthropic Engineering: "New in Claude Managed Agents: dreaming, outcomes, and multiagent orchestration"

**English:**
Anthropic shipped a major update to Claude Managed Agents with four significant new capabilities. (1) Dreaming: a scheduled process that reviews agent sessions, extracts patterns, and curates memories so agents self-improve across sessions. Developers can choose automatic or human-reviewed memory updates. (2) Outcomes: a rubric-based evaluation system where a separate grader assesses outputs against defined criteria, enabling agents to self-correct until output is "good enough." Testing showed up to +10% task success on hardest problems, +8.4% on docx generation, +10.1% on pptx. (3) Multiagent orchestration: a lead agent breaks jobs into pieces and delegates to specialists with their own models and tools, working in parallel on a shared filesystem. Full trace visibility in Claude Console. (4) Webhooks for async notification. Real-world results: Harvey saw ~6x improvement in completion rates; Netflix's platform team uses it to analyze build logs across hundreds of applications in parallel; Spiral by Every uses Haiku as lead agent delegating to Opus subagents for parallel drafting, with outcomes enforcing editorial quality bar.

**中文：**
Anthropic 为 Claude Managed Agents 发布重大更新，包含四个重要新能力。①Dreaming：一个定时进程，审查 Agent Session、提取模式、整理记忆，让 Agent 跨 Session 自我改进。开发者可选择自动更新或人工审查。②Outcomes：基于评分标准的评估系统，由独立 grader 依据定义的标准评估输出质量，驱动 Agent 自我修正直至产出"足够好"。测试显示：最难问题任务成功率提升 10%，docx 生成提升 8.4%，pptx 提升 10.1%。③Multi-agent orchestration：主 Agent 将任务分解并委托给各有专属模型和工具的专业子 Agent，在共享文件系统上并行工作。Claude Console 提供完整执行追踪。④Webhook 异步通知。真实效果：Harvey 任务完成率提升约 6 倍；Netflix 平台团队用它并行分析数百应用的构建日志；Every 的 Spiral 以 Haiku 为主 Agent 委托 Opus 子 Agent 并行起草，用 Outcomes 强制执行编辑质量标准。

### Unsupervised Learning: "Ep 87: Gemini Co-Lead on World Models, RL's Next Domains & Continual Learning"

**English:**
Oriol Vinyals, Google Gemini Co-Lead alongside Jeff Dean, sat down with Unsupervised Learning host Jacob Efron for a wide-ranging post-I/O conversation. Vinyals discussed his view that video/image understanding hasn't yet had its "GPT moment" — the pure transfer of knowledge from pixels without language correlation remains unsolved. On world models: Omni isn't just a video model; it combines representation learning with language-conditioned rendering, enabling precise simulation control via natural language. On robotics: the key gap is not visual understanding but tactile precision and force modeling — modalities we don't yet have training data for. On memory: the most practical near-term solution is file-system-based non-parametric memory rather than integrating into weights; different users will have different knowledge bases without needing different model weights. On the Bitter Lesson: he believes complex scaffolding (multi-agents, sub-agents, delegation) will eventually be written on the fly by models themselves. Most candid admission: "If seven years ago I had a model like what we have now, would I call it AGI? Probably yes." What's still missing in his view: the ability to truly learn from experience, to generalize from a few examples like humans do.

**中文：**
Google Gemini 联合负责人 Oriol Vinyals 与 Unsupervised Learning 主持人 Jacob Efron 进行了一场 I/O 之后的广泛对话。Vinyals 阐述了他的观点：视频/图像理解尚未迎来"GPT 时刻"——在没有语言关联的情况下从像素中纯粹迁移知识的问题仍未解决。关于世界模型：Omni 不只是一个视频模型，它将表征学习与语言条件渲染结合，通过自然语言实现精确的模拟控制。关于机器人技术：关键差距不在视觉理解而在触觉精度和力学建模——这些我们还没有训练数据的模态。关于记忆：最实用的近期解决方案是基于文件系统的非参数记忆，而非整合进模型权重；不同用户可以有不同知识库而无需不同模型权重。关于 Bitter Lesson：他认为复杂的脚手架（多 Agent、子 Agent、委托）最终会由模型自己动态编写。最坦诚的承认："如果七年前我有一个像现在这样的模型，我会叫它 AGI 吗？可能会的。"他认为仍然缺失的能力：真正从经验中学习的能力，像人类一样从少数样本中泛化的能力。
