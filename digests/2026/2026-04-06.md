### Andrej Karpathy (Former Director of AI @ Tesla, Founding Team @ OpenAI)

**On Personal AI Wikis and Data Sovereignty: "File over App"**

Karpathy highlighted "Farzapedia" as a compelling example of personal knowledge management that puts users in full control:

1. **Explicit memory** — Knowledge exists as a navigable wiki; you can see exactly what the AI knows and doesn't
2. **Data ownership** — Files live locally, not trapped in a vendor's system
3. **File over app** — Universal formats (images, Markdown) mean any tool can process the data; agents can apply the entire Unix toolkit natively
4. **BYOAI** — Use any AI (Claude, Codex, etc.) over your data; in principle, fine-tune an open-source model to "know" you in its weights

His verdict: this approach to personalization keeps AI companies on their toes, and "agent proficiency" is a core skill of the 21st century.

**On AI for Government Accountability: Transparency in Reverse**

Karpathy is bullish on AI empowering citizens to hold governments accountable. Historically, governments made society "legible" (per "Seeing like a State"), but AI enables the reverse. The bottleneck was never access to data — governments publish enormous amounts — but the intelligence to process it. A 4,000-page omnibus bill is "transparent" legally, but practically incomprehensible to most people. AI could dissolve this bottleneck: detailed spending audits, legislative diffs, lobbying influence graphs, regulatory capture warning lights, campaign finance patterns.

He acknowledges the same tools cut both ways, but leans optimistic that added participation and transparency will strengthen democratic societies.

他的评价是：这种个性化方式让 AI 公司保持警觉，而"Agent 能力"将成为 21 世纪的核心技能。

### Aaron Levie (CEO @ Box)

**Why the Context Layer is the Core of the Enterprise AI Stack**

Levie articulates a fundamental constraint of applied AI in the enterprise: even the most capable models can't contain all the knowledge needed to be useful, because every user has different access levels to corporate information. In a single banking team, different bankers are permitted to see entirely different documents — and sanitizing this is hard, while making a model "keep secrets" is near-impossible.

This is why continual learning at the model layer — even within a single enterprise — is nearly impossible. The implication: **the context layer will always be the core part of the AI stack for applied use cases**. You can't fight the physics on this one.

https://x.com/levie/status/2040650799550722243

**How AI Agents Are Finally Starting to Work Like Humans, Not Like Fragments**

Levie traces the evolution of document-processing agents: a year ago, the state of the art was embedding-based similarity search — chunk documents, find matches, send to the model. This worked surprisingly well but had fundamental limits (e.g., a document that says "do not use this" on page 1 but has relevant info on page 3 would send the relevant info as a top hit).

Today, with larger context windows, better tool calling, and improved reasoning, agents can work more like people: semantic search across entire document sets, reading many documents simultaneously, and — critically — knowing when to search again when something feels off. These improvements prompted a complete architectural rethink for Box Agent.

https://x.com/levie/status/2040528801386361128

亚伦·莱维（Box CEO）

**为什么 Context Layer 是企业 AI 架构的核心**

莱维阐明了企业 AI 应用的一个根本约束：即使是最强大的模型，也无法拥有回答所有问题所需的全部知识——因为每个用户对企业信息的访问权限都完全不同。在一个银行团队中，不同职员能看到截然不同的文件，而理清这些权限边界非常困难，让模型"保守秘密"几乎不可能。

这意味着：即使在单个企业内部，模型层面的持续学习也几乎不可能实现。结论是：**Context Layer（上下文层）将始终是 AI 应用架构的核心部分**。这是物理约束，无法绕过。

https://x.com/levie/status/2040650799550722243

**AI Agent 如何终于开始像人一样工作，而不是像碎片一样拼凑**

莱维回顾了文档处理 Agent 的演进：一年前，最先进的方法是基于嵌入的相似性搜索——将文档分块、找匹配项、发送给模型。这种方法在约束条件下效果出奇好，但有根本性局限（例如，文档第 1 页写着"不要使用"但第 3 页有相关信息，相似性搜索会把相关信息作为最高匹配项发出去）。

如今，随着 context window 变大、tool calling 变强、推理能力提升，Agent 可以更像人一样工作了：跨整个文档集做语义搜索、同时阅读大量文档，更重要的是——在发现某些信息不对劲时主动重新检索。这些改进促使 Box 在设计 Box Agent 时完全重构了架构。

### No Priors | Liam Fedus (Co-creator of ChatGPT, Co-founder @ Periodic Labs)

**From Language AI to Atomic AI: Why the Physical World is the Next Frontier**

Liam Fedus was VP of Post Training at OpenAI, where he worked on ChatGPT's creation. Before that at Google Brain, he was present for the invention of the transformer and the early scaling era. He now runs Periodic Labs — an AI foundation lab for atoms, applying AI to materials science, chemistry, and physics.

**The Takeaway:**

> "Science ultimately isn't sitting in a room thinking really hard. You have to conduct experiments, you have to learn from them, you have to interface with reality. The creation of ChatGPT was incredible technology, but it was still far too weak. We couldn't have built Periodic on 2022-era AI."

**Why physics scientists need AI now:**

- **Data quality crisis**: When engineers examined reported material properties from literature, values spanned many orders of magnitude. Training ML on this just models the noise distribution — you get no closer to ground truth without experimental grounding
- **Closed-loop superiority**: AI directing experiments → experiments generating data → data guiding the next round of experiments is far more powerful than accumulating a static data pool
- **Cross-scale generalization**: Models that understand quantum mechanics don't directly transfer to fluid dynamics, but principles like chemical bonding and Van der Waals forces show surprising consistency across domains

**Architecture:** Language models serve as the **orchestration layer** — directing specialized, symmetry-aware neural nets (low latency, fine-tuned for atomic systems) as tools, while ingesting literature, experimental data, and other modalities.

**On AGI:** Fedus observes that AI capabilities exhibit strange "spikiness" — world-class on one math domain but degraded by simple perturbations. He believes recursive self-improvement is already happening in software engineering (highly verifiable, near-instant feedback) and slowly in AI research (requires GPUs, longer experimental cycles). Connecting these systems to the physical world via closed-loop experimentation is what will unlock the rest.

**The vision:**
> "Imagine we go from systems that aren't just writing essays and code, but literally generating matter. If the physical world could keep up at some fraction of the pace of our digital world — life would just feel dramatically different."

诺·普里奥尔斯播客 | 利亚姆·费杜斯（ChatGPT 联合创始人、Periodic Labs 联合创始人）

**从语言 AI 到原子 AI：为什么物理世界才是下一个主战场**

利亚姆·费杜斯曾在 OpenAI 担任 Post Training 副总裁，参与了 ChatGPT 的创建。在此之前，他在 Google Brain 经历了 Transformer 的发明和早期 scaling 时代。如今他创办了 Periodic Labs——一家面向原子的 AI 基础实验室，将 AI 应用于材料科学、化学和物理学。

**核心结论：**

> "科学最终不是坐在房间里使劲思考。你必须做实验，必须从中学习，必须与现实世界互动。ChatGPT 的诞生是一项了不起的技术，但它当时还太弱。在 2022 年的 AI 技术上，我们根本做不了 Periodic 的事。"

**为什么物理科学家现在需要 AI：**

- **数据质量危机**：工程师审查文献中报告的材料参数时，发现数值差异极大（横跨多个数量级）。在这样的数据上训练 ML，只是在建模噪声分布——没有实验 grounding，就不可能接近真相
- **闭环系统的威力**：AI 指导实验 → 实验产生数据 → 数据驱动下一轮实验，比单纯积累静态数据池强大得多
- **跨尺度泛化**：理解量子力学的模型不能直接迁移到流体动力学，但化学键、Vander Waals 力等原理在跨领域上展现出惊人的一致性

**架构设计**：语言模型作为**编排层（orchestration layer）**，指挥专用、symmetry-aware 神经网络（低延迟，为原子系统 fine-tuned）作为工具，同时整合文献、实验数据等多种模态。

**关于 AGI**：费杜斯观察到 AI 能力表现出奇怪的"刺突性"——在某个数学领域是世界级的，但简单扰动就能大幅降级。他认为递归自我改进已在软件工程领域发生（高度可验证、几乎即时的反馈），在 AI 研究领域稍慢（需要 GPU、更长的实验周期）。通过闭环实验系统将这些 AI 连接到物理世界，是解锁其余一切的关键。

**愿景：**
> "想象我们不只是写文章、写代码，而是**生成物质**。如果物理世界能以数字世界几分之一的速度发展——生活将会完全不同。"
