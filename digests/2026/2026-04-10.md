## OFFICIAL BLOGS

### Anthropic Engineering — "Scaling Managed Agents: Decoupling the brain from the hands"

The core architectural insight: decoupling the "brain" (Claude + harness) from the "hands" (sandbox execution environments) and the "session" (append-only event log) into independent interfaces. Each can fail and be replaced independently. This decoupled architecture cut p50 TTFT by ~60% and p95 by over 90%. The session is not Claude's context window—it's a durable event log that lives outside context, allowing the brain to interrogate context by selecting positional slices of the event stream at any point.

核心架构洞察：将"大脑"（Claude + harness）、"手"（sandbox 执行环境）和"会话"（仅可追加的事件日志）解耦为独立接口。每个都可以独立失败和替换。这种解耦架构将 p50 TTFT 降低约 60%，p95 降低超过 90%。会话不是 Claude 的 context window——它是驻留在 context 外部的持久事件日志，允许大脑随时通过选择事件流的 position slice 来查询 context。

https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/managed-agents
