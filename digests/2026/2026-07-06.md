### No Priors: OpenAI Noam Brown — Test-Time Compute 为什么让现有 Benchmark 全失效

OpenAI Research Scientist Noam Brown（AI reasoning 领域奠基人之一）

**The Takeaway：** 当模型能力已经成为"你投入多少 token/成本"的函数时，传统静态 Benchmark 网格本质上是一个全行业都在假装没问题的"坏均衡"——所有人都知道它有问题，但没人愿意先打破它。

当前 Benchmark 体系正在系统性误导行业。Noam Brown 在这篇深度访谈中指出：当模型能力已经成为"你投入多少 token/成本"的函数时，传统静态 Benchmark 网格（二维坐标轴对比不同模型）本质上是一个"坏均衡"——所有人都知道它有问题，但没人愿意先打破它。

The current benchmark system is systematically misleading the entire industry. In this deep-dive interview, Noam Brown points out that as model capabilities have become a function of how much token/compute budget you throw at them, the traditional static benchmark grid is essentially a "bad equilibrium" that the entire industry is pretending isn't broken.

---

**核心论点：GPT-5.5 被低估的真相**

GPT-5.5 在 Benchmark Grid 上仅比 5.4 高出几个百分点，因此遭到社区质疑。但真正的原因是：5.5 的思考效率远高于 5.4——如果控制相同的思考时间/成本，5.5 是实质性跨越。然而当前评测根本没有把"用了多少 test-time compute"作为变量纳入考量。

GPT-5.5 only showed a few percentage points improvement over 5.4 on the benchmark grid, leading to community skepticism. But the real reason: 5.5 is far more efficient with its thinking. When controlling for the same thinking time/compute, 5.5 represents a substantial leap. Yet current evaluations don't factor in how much test-time compute was used as a variable at all.

---

**现代 Benchmark 的不可评估困境**

更严峻的是：现代模型在某些 Benchmark 上可以连续思考数周性能才会趋于平稳。这意味着：真正完整的评测在实操上几乎不可行（等模型跑完数周，下一代模型已经发布了）。AISI 的研究显示，某些安全评估甚至在 1 亿 token 之后仍在提升——唯一完整的评测方法是跑满 1 亿 token。

Modern models can think for weeks before performance plateaus on certain benchmarks. This means truly complete evaluations are practically infeasible—by the time a model finishes running for weeks, the next generation has already been released. AISI's research shows some safety evaluations continue improving even after 100 million tokens—the only complete evaluation method would be running to full 100 million tokens.

---

**被忽视的隐藏红利**

GPT-5.5 已经在足够 scaffolding 下证明 Erdos 单位距离猜想——这个数学难题数学家研究了几十年。OpenAI 用很低预算做到了，但普通人只要愿意投入 $1,000–$100,000 的 token 成本，也可以用 5.5 做到同样的事。没有人真正探索过"给当前模型 $10 万预算能做什么"这个命题。

GPT-5.5 has already proven the Erdos unit distance conjecture with sufficient scaffolding—a mathematical problem mathematicians spent decades on. OpenAI did it at a low budget, but ordinary people could do the same with 5.5 if willing to spend $1,000–$100,000 in token costs. Nobody has truly explored what current models can do with a $100,000 budget.

---

**关于递归自我提升（RSI）**

Noam 认为"快速起飞"（几天内模型自我迭代到超级智能）不太可能发生，因为当前模型需要大量 test-time compute 才能达到极限——时间本身就是瓶颈。他将此描述为"我们都在疯狂工作，因为所有人都看到了 overhang，但唯一制约我们的是时间本身"。

Noam believes "fast takeoff" (models recursively self-improving to superhuman intelligence within days) is unlikely, because current models need massive test-time compute to reach their limits—and time itself is the bottleneck. He describes it as: "We're all working frantically because everyone sees the capability overhang, but the only constraint on us is time itself."

---

**最犀利的观点：Routing Layer 的compute账**

"当你控制住 test-time compute 后，你会发现那些 routing layer 的所谓优势——比如多模型 consensus——是否真的比让同一个模型多想一会儿更好？这才是正确的问题。"

"When you control for test-time compute, you'll find that the supposed advantages of routing layers—like multi-model consensus—are they actually better than just having the same model think longer? That's the right question to ask."

---

**Industry Implications / 行业影响**

- 全行业 Benchmark 发布方式需要重构：X轴应该是 token 数量/成本，而非简单模型名称对比
- Safety evaluation 的时间窗口与模型发布节奏存在根本性错配
- 模型能力 overhang 比想象中更大：现有模型 + 高 compute 预算可以做到很多未被探索的事
- 时间将成为所有 frontier labs 的共同瓶颈
