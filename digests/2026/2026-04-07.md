### Chris Manning (Stanford NLP 终身教授、Moonlake 联合创始人)

Manning 和 Moonlake另一位联合创始人 Fan-yun Sun 在 Latent Space Podcast 中阐述了他们的核心观点：当前 Sora、Genie 等视频生成模型虽然视觉精美，但本质上缺乏对3D物理世界、物体运动规律和因果关系的理解。他们主张 **"结构而非规模"（Structure, Not Scale）**——用符号化、抽象的语义层来表示世界，而非直接在像素级别操作。以更少的数据量（比纯像素方法少5个数量级）实现更高效的推理和学习。

Manning 特别批评了 LeCun 的 JEPA 路线："语言是人类设计的抽象表示系统，每个 token 都承载语义——这与从像素逐帧学习是完全不同的数据形态。"他认为人脑也大量依赖语义抽象而非像素级处理，这从神经科学角度支持了 Moonlake 的方法论。

Moonlake 的世界模型将代码/游戏引擎作为可调用的"认知工具"，在需要物理模拟时调用物理引擎，需要其他表示时灵活切换——这是与传统端到端像素预测的本质区别。

Chris Manning, longtime Stanford NLP professor and co-founder of Moonlake, joined the Latent Space podcast with co-founder Fan-yun Sun to present their core thesis: current video generation models like Sora and Genie look visually stunning but fundamentally lack understanding of 3D physics, object behavior, and causal relationships. They advocate **"Structure, Not Scale"** — using symbolic, abstracted semantic representations rather than operating at the pixel level, which enables far more efficient learning with orders of magnitude less data than pure pixel-based approaches.

Manning specifically critiqued LeCun's JEPA approach: "Language is a human-designed abstracted representation where there's meaning in each token — it's representing an abstraction of the world, not at the pixel level." He argued the human brain also relies heavily on semantic abstraction over pixel-level processing, which from a neuroscience perspective supports Moonlake's methodology.

Moonlake's world model treats code/game engines as callable "cognitive tools" — invoking a physics engine when needed for physical simulation, or switching to other representations flexibly — fundamentally different from traditional end-to-end pixel prediction.

关键洞察："二阶效应才是关键——AI Agent 让工作更高效，会诱导对这类技能需求的增长。大多数关于 AI 减少工作岗位的预测都不会成真。"

Aaron Levie, CEO of Box, offered a counterintuitive perspective on AI and employment. He argues that AI agents making work more efficient will actually **induce demand** for that skill in most categories, rather than eliminate jobs. The core logic: AI makes coding easier → businesses apply software to more parts of their operations → more engineers needed. AI makes video production easier → more media content created → more media management roles. AI lowers the barrier for legal document creation → more complex bespoke legal documents → more lawyers.

Key insight: "The second-order effects are where it matters — AI agents making work more efficient will induce demand for that skill. Most predictions about AI eliminating jobs won't play out as advertised."

### Latent Space: Moonlake — Causal World Models Should Be Multimodal, Interactive, and Efficient

**嘉宾：Chris Manning（Stanford 教授、Moonlake 联合创始人）+ Fan-yun Sun（Moonlake 联合创始人）**

**核心洞察：**

Moonlake 正在解决 AI 领域一个根本性问题：当前视频生成模型（Sora、World Labs Marble 等）只能"欣赏"世界，却无法与世界中物体**互动**并看到正确的行为后果。他们构建的世界模型是**动作条件（action-conditioned）**的——给定一个动作输入，模型预测世界如何变化。

**Structure Not Scale** 是核心哲学：与其收集海量像素级视频数据期待涌现因果理解，不如从一开始就建立符号化、语义化的世界表示。他们用"游戏引擎作为认知工具"——让模型在需要物理模拟时调用物理引擎，需要几何推理时调用几何工具，而不是所有东西都从像素中学习。

**与 LeCun 的 JEPA 路线分歧**：Manning 认为 LeCun"严重低估了语言的力量"。人类相比黑猩猩的认知飞跃来自语言和符号表示，而非视觉系统的提升。"我们用来做空间推理和规划的，正是语言赋予的抽象因果推理链。"

**下一步**：Moonlake 计划将世界模型用于游戏引擎可编辑内容（类似皮肤/外观层）、机器人训练/评估、以及任何需要在多模态环境中执行策略的领域。他们相信"数据飞轮"模式——将工具交给创作者，让他们教会模型需要改进什么能力。

Moonlake is tackling a fundamental problem in AI: current video generation models (Sora, World Labs Marble, etc.) can only "appreciate" the world but cannot interact with objects and see the correct consequences of actions. Their world model is **action-conditioned** — given an action input, the model predicts how the world changes.

**Structure Not Scale** is the core philosophy: instead of collecting massive amounts of pixel-level video data hoping causal understanding emerges, build symbolic, semantic world representations from the start. They use "game engines as cognitive tools" — letting the model invoke a physics engine when it needs physics simulation, or geometry tools when needed, rather than learning everything from pixels.

**The split with LeCun's JEPA approach**: Manning believes LeCun "fundamentally misses a number of important things" about language. Humans' cognitive leap over chimpanzees came from language and symbolic representations, not improvements to the visual system. "Language is the cognitive tool that gave us the ability to think in abstractions and extended causal reasoning chains."

**What's next**: Moonlake plans to use world models for game engine editable content (like skins/layers), robot training/evaluation, and any domain requiring policies to act in multimodal environments. They believe in the "data flywheel" model — put tools in creators' hands and let them teach the model what capabilities need improvement.
