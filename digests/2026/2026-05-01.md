### AI & I by Every — "How Stripe Is Building for an Agent-native World"

Stripe sees 2% of global GDP, giving them the clearest view of the emerging agent economy. In this episode, Emily Glassberg Sands (Head of Data & AI at Stripe) sits down with Dan Shipper to discuss what's actually happening at the earliest innings of the agent economy.

**Key Insights:**

• **AI companies are scaling faster than any cohort Stripe has tracked.** Top AI companies reach $30M ARR in ~18 months — 3x faster than the top 100 SaaS companies from 2018. This growth is primarily net-new spend, not cannibalized software budgets.

• **Fraud is evolving from stolen cards to stolen compute.** The new fraud vectors: multi-account abuse (7% of signups are fraudulent), free trial abuse (4x growth in 6 months, one customer blocking 250K fraudulent trials/week), and non-payment abuse where customers rack up thousands in compute and never pay. "In AI, every prompt, every image generated, every API request has a very real cost attached to it."

• **Seat-based billing is becoming "a little silly" for AI tools.** If agents make each developer 10x more productive, why would you peg revenue to developer count? Vertical AI will eventually converge on outcome-based pricing, not tokens or seats.

• **The Agent-to-Commerce Protocol enables agents to buy on behalf of consumers.** Built with OpenAI, used by Microsoft Copilot and Meta's ad shopping experience. Merchants integrate once and can be exposed across multiple AI storefronts. Consumers use Stripe Link for delegated purchasing with guardrails.

• **Stripe Radar now covers the full customer lifecycle.** Fraud detection used to happen at checkout. Now it's at sign-up (is this multi-account abuse?), during trials (should they get credits?), and at overages (should we throttle or require top-up?).

Emily 还透露了一个具体案例：某大型 AI 公司免费试用转化率仅 4%，每次试用成本 $25，算下来每个付费用户获取成本 $625——而绝大多数试用者实际上是欺诈者。

**Quote:** "The Internet has this new kind of actor on it. Over time, these agents will become the predominant actors on the Internet."

Stripe 看到了 2% 的全球 GDP，有最清晰的 Agent 经济视图。在本期节目中，Stripe 数据与 AI 负责人 Emily Glassberg Sands 与 Dan Shipper 讨论了 Agent 经济最早期的真实状况。

**核心要点：**

• **AI 公司增速刷新历史记录。** 顶尖 AI 公司约 18 个月达到 3000 万 ARR，是 2018 年 SaaS 公司的 3 倍。增长主要来自净新增预算，而非蚕食传统软件。

• **欺诈正在升级：从盗卡到偷算力。** 新欺诈场景包括：多账号滥用（7% 的注册都是欺诈）、免费试用欺诈（6 个月增长 4 倍，某客户每周拦截 25 万次欺诈试用）、欠费跑路（用户积累数千美元算力成本后不付款）。"在 AI 中，每个提示、每张生成的图片、每次 API 请求都有真实的成本。"

• **座位制计费对 AI 工具来说正在变得"有点荒谬"。** 如果 Agent 让每个开发者效率提升 10 倍，为什么按开发者数量来计算收入？垂直 AI 最终会收敛到基于成果的计费，而非 token 或座位。

• **Agent-to-Commerce 协议让 Agent 代表消费者购买。** 与 OpenAI 共创，被 Microsoft Copilot 和 Meta 广告购物体验采用。商家只需集成一次，即可在多个 AI 平台露出。消费者通过 Stripe Link 实现带保护机制的委托购买。

• **Stripe Radar 现在覆盖客户全生命周期。** 欺诈检测曾经发生在结账时，现在在注册时（这是多账号滥用吗？）、试用时（应该给他们试用资格吗？）和超额时（我们应该限流还是要求预付？）都要做判断。

**金句：** "互联网上出现了一种新的参与者。随着时间的推移，这些 Agent 将成为互联网的主要参与者。"
